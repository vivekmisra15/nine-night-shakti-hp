# Fact-check (18 October 2026)

Every word in the app is copied from the docs, so the docs were checked: all nine night files (lesson, read-aloud script and sources), the plan (`NINE_NIGHTS_CONTENT.md`), the parent prep sheet, the disclaimer and the map's names. About 800 claims were checked against primary sources where possible: Shiva Purana (J. L. Shastri, on wisdomlib), Bhagavata Purana (vedabase.io), Devi Mahatmya (Aurobharati and the standard 700-verse text), Skanda Purana (Tagare), Kumarasambhava (GRETIL), the Mahabharata (Ganguli); for Harry Potter, the books' chapters, the *Muggles' Guide*, the HP Lexicon and film transcripts. A spending limit cut some web research short, so a few checks rest on the checker's knowledge of the texts; each is marked "from knowledge" in the detailed reports.

**Update, 18 October 2026:** all 3 High and 14 Medium findings have been fixed, with Supriya's approval; every change is logged in `docs/fact-check/CHANGES.md`. The 60 Low findings are still open. The detailed reports, with evidence links and suggested wording for every item (including the low ones), are in `docs/fact-check/night-N.md` and `docs/fact-check/series.md`.

## Overall

The traditional stories are very accurate. Almost every chapter and verse reference checked out, including the Devi Mahatmya verse by verse (Nights 6, 7, 9), the Shiva Purana (Nights 2, 3, 5), the Bhagavata Purana (Nights 1, 9) and the Skanda Purana (Night 8). All Harry Potter chapter numbers and titles, film years and Devanagari spellings are correct. Most problems are small slips in the Harry Potter retellings and film notes (several in text shortened or written in Phase A), and a few citations.

## Low (60)

Mostly wording and small details, each with a suggested fix in the detailed reports. Some worth knowing:

- **Gentle alternative retellings written in Phase A:**
  - Night 7's Grim has Harry see the dog turn into Sirius (he finds him already in human form), and reads as if Harry learns there that Sirius is his godfather (he knew from ch. 10).
  - Night 2's activity invents a letter "in November".
- **Night 9, Beat 13:** quotes Quirrell's line almost word for word, more than the "few words" rule allows.
- **Text written this week:**
  - The disclaimer's book list leaves out *Half-Blood Prince*.
  - It says the Devi is "shown only as light, never as drawn figures", which predates the agreed faint outline.
- **Map meanings (`journey.ts`):**
  - Kushmanda creates the cosmic egg rather than being "the warm cosmic egg".
  - "Born of the gods' combined fury" is Katyayani's origin; her name means "of the sage Katyayana".
- **Prep sheet:**
  - Kreacher's tale is "Some peril" there but "Gentle" in the plan.
  - The *Deathly Hallows Part 1/2* film titles are written three ways.
