# Night 6 direction: Katyayani, born of the gods' combined fury

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-6.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "ash, gathered fire, lion-gold dawn".** The gods' exile is ash grey and dusk indigo. Their many lights are each a different soft colour, and they merge into one saffron-gold blaze, the Devi. The battle is that gold against a deep smoke-violet shadow. The night ends in a clear gold dawn.
- **Signature motif: many small lights becoming one.** It is the gods' lights flowing together in Beat 4. It returns, smaller, in the Hogwarts scenes: candle points gathering in the Hog's Head (Beat 9) and three separate lights passing one another along a corridor (Beat 11). At the close, the gathered light settles into a single diya, which becomes diya 6 on the map.
- **The Devi:** warm saffron-gold light. At the moment of her forming (Beat 4) and in Beat 7, a very faint fine-line head-and-shoulders outline shows within her light (three small points for her eyes at most; no other features, never a body). Her symbols are small gold outline icons.
- **The gods:** formless light. Shiva cool silver, Vishnu soft blue, Brahma soft rose-gold, Indra pale lightning white, Agni ember orange, Yama deep violet, Varuna aqua, Vayu pale mint. The others are small white-gold points.
- **Ending:** the shadow of the buffalo breaks apart into dawn (Beat 6); the night closes on that dawn, with the theme over it.

**Ragas** (comment each choice in the score code)

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |

| Bhupali | Serene devotion, open | Opening, closing | The series' home raga; the night begins and ends in devotion. |

| Durga (new) | Bright, steady, heroic (S R M P D, pentatonic) | Beats 4 and 7 | A strong, uncomplicated brightness for the Devi taking form, distinct from Bhupali's calm. Needs a new scale in the engine. |

| Yaman | Tender evening, reflective | Beats 8, 13–15 | Warm and thoughtful for the theme and the closing talk. |

**Hogwarts scenes (Beats 9–12):** the original palette only: celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings, low woodwind, D Dorian, stepwise motifs in a narrow range. Never imitate any film theme; no 3/8 waltz.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: night sky over a low ridge; a soft temple bell, then a diya lights at centre (the series invocation). On 1.2, faint points of coloured light appear scattered across the sky, not yet joined. Camera: pullBack. Intensity 2. Palette `gatheredLight` (dimmed). Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm, drone + motif; bell at start.

**Beat 2: Heaven lost** — picture: sentence 0, two hosts of light and smoke clash far off on a ridge line, distant and small; sentence 2, the lights dim; sentence 3, a throne outline high in the clouds, a smoke shape settling on it as Indra's white light slips away; sentence 4, sun and moon icons fade under smoke; sentence 5, the gods' lights drift low and scattered across a grey plain. Final frame: small dim lights wandering under an ash sky. Camera: aerial, slow drift down. Freeze after sentence 5. Intensity 4. Palette `exile`. Music: indian, Bageshri, 60 bpm, drone + strings; soft occasional gusts.

**Beat 3: What should the gods do?** — backdrop: Beat 2's final frame, breathing. Reveal: the lights gather and travel upward to a rose-gold glow (Brahma), then to silver and blue (Shiva, Vishnu). Intensity 3. Music: Bageshri, drone only; motif returns at the reveal.

**Beat 4: Born of light** — picture: sentence 0, the silver and blue lights flare; sentence 1, rays pour from them and from the rose-gold light; sentence 2, every god's coloured light streams inward from all sides (the signature motif); sentence 3, they merge into a towering blaze, a mountain of fire; sentences 4–5, the blaze softens into warm gold, and the faint head-and-shoulders outline shows within it, then the light floods the frame; sentences 6–8, threads of colour (silver, blue, ember) settle into her light; sentence 9, all lights brighten; sentences 10–17, each gift arrives as a small gold outline icon flying from its god's light to circle hers (trident, discus, conch and noose, spear, bow, thunderbolt and bell, staff, rosary and water pot, sword and shield, lotus, axe, lion, cup, serpent necklace); sentences 18–20, the trident and discus are shown drawn out of a larger one, which stays. Final frame: her light ringed by the icons. Camera: pushIn, then crane up at sentence 3. Slow motion: sentence 2, about 4 s, as the lights flow together. Freeze after sentence 5. Intensity 7. Palette `gatheredLight`. Music: indian, Durga, 72 bpm, drone + motif + strings, swelling to climax at sentence 3; conch at sentence 4; a small bell with each gift.

**Beat 5: "What is this?"** — backdrop: her light pulses with the roar (a soft concentric ripple; mountains tremble slightly); far below, a smoke shape on the stolen throne stirs. Reveal: the smoke surges up the slope with ember points (the army); her light fills the frame top to bottom. Intensity 6. Music: Marwa, 80 bpm, drone + percussion; a low drum hit at the reveal.

**Beat 6: The battle** — picture: sentence 0, waves of smoke shapes with ember points roll towards her light; sentence 1, sparks breathe out from her light and become a host of small lights; sentence 2, the lion's gold outline runs through the smoke, which parts; sentence 3, two large smoke shapes dissolve; sentences 4–5, a vast buffalo shadow rises, the ground line shaking; sentence 6, a gold noose icon loops around it; sentences 7–8, the shadow changes in silhouette: lion, man with a sword, elephant, buffalo again, her light meeting each; sentences 9–11, a small gold cup icon brightens, her light ripples with laughter; sentence 12, her light rises above the shadow and the trident icon flashes once; sentences 13–14, the shadow breaks apart into drifting smoke and then into dawn light (no figure, no wound, no blood); sentences 15–16, remaining smoke flees off frame, the gods' lights gather and brighten, sage and musician outlines with raised hands; sentences 17–18, her light fades gently away. Final frame: an empty gold dawn. Camera: lowAngle, then pullBack at sentence 14. Slow motion: sentence 7, about 5 s, for the shape changes. Freeze after sentence 8 and after sentence 14. Intensity 9 (peak at sentences 12–14), easing to 4. Palette `battleShadow`, crossing to `lionDawn` at sentence 14. Music: indian, Marwa, 96 bpm, all layers, climax at sentence 12 set to f, not ff; at sentence 15 switch to resolution (temple bell, warm pad). Ambience: soft occasional gusts; no battle noise bed. Parent bell: pulses at the start and again before sentence 12.

**Beat 7: Katyayani** — picture: dawn; a quiet riverbank and a small hermitage outline (a seated sage outline with topknot) as her light settles there; sentence for the cowherd girls: small veiled outlines at the river's edge with diyas. The six symbols (lion, sword, lotus, raised palm, lowered palm, third eye) appear as gold icons around her light, with the faint outline within it; they are the tappable hotspots. Camera: drift. Intensity 4. Palette `lionDawn`. Music: indian, Durga, 64 bpm, drone + motif; water in a soft trickle.

**Beat 8: The theme** — backdrop: many small coloured lights drawn faintly, flowing into one gold flame. Intensity 2. Palette `reflective`. Music: Yaman, drone + soft motif.

**Beat 9: Dumbledore's Army** — picture, with letterbox bars: sentence 0, a closed book on a desk, no light; sentence 1, a sheet of newsprint curling in shadow; sentence 2, a single candle lit; sentence 3, the candle wavers; sentence 4, a low pub doorway, then candle points appearing one by one around a table until there are many; sentence 5, a parchment, a line of small lights running down it as names are added; sentence 6, a blank stone wall where a door slowly outlines itself in light; sentence 7, warm room, the many small lights rising together, and one bright spark (the Disarming Charm). Final frame: many lights in one room. Camera: still, then slow pushIn at sentence 4. Freeze after sentence 5. Intensity 5. Palette `hogsHead`. Music: hogwarts, 66 bpm, drone (low strings) + celesta; harp joins at sentence 4; soft fire crackle in the pub.

**Beat 10: Think it through** — backdrop: the room of many small lights, dimmed. Intensity 1. Music: hogwarts drone only; activity ducking and timer tick.

**Beat 11: Through the trapdoor** — picture, with letterbox bars: sentence 0, a trapdoor in a stone floor, three small lights at its edge; sentence 1, dark ink tendrils curl and retreat from a burst of flame; sentence 2, a room of fluttering key shapes, one light rises and catches one; sentence 3, a chessboard of stone squares, one light steps forward and goes dim; sentence 4, a row of small bottles in torchlight; sentence 5, two lights part, one going back; sentence 6, a single light goes on down a corridor; sentence 7, a long hall of floating candles, and one small light set apart brightens last. Final frame: the candle hall. Camera: crane down at sentence 1, then pan. Slow motion: sentence 3, about 3 s. Freeze after sentence 3. Intensity 5. Palette `trapdoor`, crossing to `greatHall` at sentence 7. Music: hogwarts, 72 bpm, drone + low woodwind + harp; a celesta chime at sentence 7.

**Beat 12: Think it through** — backdrop: the stone chessboard, dimmed. Intensity 1. Music: hogwarts drone only.

**Beat 13: Side by side** — backdrop: faint gathered gold light beside a faint candle hall. Intensity 2. Palette `reflective`. Music: Yaman, drone.

**Beat 14: Today** — backdrop: faint night sky over a ridge. Intensity 2. Music: Yaman, drone + soft motif.

**Beat 15: Reflection** — backdrop: faint night sky with one diya; at 15.2 the sky begins to lighten. Intensity 2. Music: Yaman into Bhupali for the closing; temple bell, 5 s tanpura tail.

## New drawing pieces

- **GodLight:** the DivineLight kit extended with a colour per god (rose-gold, lightning white, ember, violet, aqua, mint) and a "stream inward" path for many lights to merge.
- **DeviOutline:** the faint fine-line head-and-shoulders outline inside a flame, for use across later nights.
- **SymbolIcon additions:** sword, shield, discus, conch, noose, spear, bow, thunderbolt, bell, staff, rosary, water pot, axe, cup, serpent necklace, lion, open palm (raised and lowered), third eye.

- **Lion outline:** a running gold line-art lion.
- **Throne outline** (in clouds), **hermitage outline**, **river edge**.
- **Hogwarts objects:** newsprint sheet, parchment with names as lights, a door outlining itself in a wall, ink tendrils, flying key shapes, stone chessboard, potion bottle row.
