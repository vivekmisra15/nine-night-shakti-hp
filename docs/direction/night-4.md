# Night 4 direction: Kushmanda, the warm cosmic egg

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-4.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "from the dark, a warm gold".** The night begins in a deep, almost starless blue-black, and every traditional beat grows warmer: honey, saffron and the white-gold of the sun's core. The ash gourd's soft grey-green appears once, in Beat 6. The Hogwarts scenes keep their own cooler storm-slate and candle-amber, so the two worlds never blur.
- **Signature motif: one point of warm light.** It is the Devi's smile in Beat 2, which swells into the cosmic egg; it becomes the heart of the sun in Beat 4; it settles into a golden egg beside the ash gourd in Beat 6; it glows faintly behind the theme in Beat 7. At the close it becomes the fourth diya on the map.
- **How the night ends visually:** a slow sunrise. The sun lifts over a low ridge line, its core holding the warm point of light, and the theme's word and idea fade in over it. Then the point of light drifts up as a spark, and the map appears with diya 4 lit.
- **Musical motif:** the bansuri plays a soft rising phrase in Bhupali, *Sa Re Ga* then a held *Pa* ("the first light"), heard whole at the smile (Beat 2), at the sun (Beat 4) and at the close. Elsewhere only fragments return. Root D, tanpura on Sa–Pa, as on Night 1. Tempo 52–72 BPM all night: there is no climax tonight, only a bloom.

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Malkauns *(new, approved 18 October)* | Deep night, stillness, the time before anything | Beat 2, sentences 0–1 only | A midnight raga with no Re or Pa; it suits "nothing to see and no one to see it", and gives way to Bhupali at the smile. |
| Bhupali | Serene devotion, open and bright | Beat 2 from the smile, Beat 4, Beat 6, closing | Its pentatonic brightness is the first light: nothing in it pulls or aches. |
| Yaman | Tender evening, warm and reflective | Beats 1, 7, 12, 13, 14 (before the close) | The night's thinking voice, as on Night 1. |
| Bageshri | Gentle longing | Not used tonight | There is no loss or longing in Kushmanda's story. |
| Marwa | Twilight unease | Not used tonight | There is no tension or peril tonight. |

- **Hogwarts scenes (Beats 8–11):** the original Hogwarts palette only: celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings and low woodwind, in D Dorian, stepwise and narrow in range. Never imitate any film theme; no 3/8 waltz, no leaping minor celesta line.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: a small gold bell icon (last night's Chandraghanta) glows and fades on sentence 0 of "Last night"; the sky darkens step by step until only a few stars remain, then those go out too as "Tonight's form" begins, leaving a dark frame with one faint warm point at centre. Camera: slow pullBack. Intensity 2. Palette `reflective` fading to `primordialDark`. Music: indian, Yaman, 56 bpm; drone, motif (one bansuri phrase); one soft temple bell at the start. Ambience: none. Parent bell: pulses once at the start of the beat.

**Beat 2: Before anything at all** — picture: sentence 0, total dark with the faintest grain of indigo haze; sentence 1, the haze stills, no stars. Sentence 2, the warm point brightens into a glow (the smile is the glow blooming, never a drawn mouth); within it, for a moment, a very light fine-line outline of a face and shoulders, then it softens back into light. Sentence 3, LightRays spread outward through the dark, and the glow gathers into a golden CosmicEgg. Sentence 4, the egg's light opens: Particles of gold drift out and become stars, then a faint Ridge line and small points of life. Sentence 5, the frame settles on the egg-glow amid the new stars; a nine-petal mandala turns once, faintly. Camera: still for sentences 0–1, then slow pushIn on the glow, then pullBack on sentence 4 to reveal the new sky. Slow motion: sentence 3, 4 s, as the light first spreads. Freeze after sentence 3 (the egg) and on the final frame. Intensity 5. Palette `primordialDark` warming to `sunCore`. Music: indian; Malkauns, drone only, 52 bpm, for sentences 0–1; a single temple bell on sentence 2, then Bhupali, 60 bpm, with the full motif (*Sa Re Ga*, held *Pa*); layers drone, motif, strings, resolution (singing bowl on sentence 4). No percussion. Ambience: none (silence is the point).

**Beat 3: What comes first?** — backdrop: the dark frame of Beat 2, sentence 0, held breathing behind the cards; at the reveal the glow returns and the egg forms again. Music: Malkauns drone under the cards; Bhupali fragment at the reveal.

**Beat 4: The one who lives in the sun** — picture: sentence 0, a SunDisc rises in the centre of the new sky with a slow corona; the warm point of light sits at its heart. Sentence 1, the heat shimmers outward and the corona deepens; the point stays calm (she alone bears it). Sentence 2, ten long rays reach out to the ten directions and light the Ridge below. Sentence 3, eight finer rays of light open from her glow like a wheel (her arms as light, not a body), each tipped with a small gold SymbolIcon; a gold outline lion settles beneath. Sentence 4, the light softens, and Lotus and Diya points appear on the land below (devotees' lamps). Final frame: the sun with eight tipped rays and the lion; icons pulse gently as hotspots on step 4.2. Camera: lowAngle, then slow crane up as the rays open; still for the symbols. Freeze after sentence 3. Intensity 5. Palette `sunCore`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 66 bpm; drone, motif (full on sentence 0), strings, light tabla (keherwa, soft), resolution; a bell on sentence 3; each hotspot tap plays one soft chime in Bhupali. Ambience: none.

**Beat 5: How much does it take?** — backdrop: the dark sky with a single spark held still; at the reveal the spark warms into the sun of Beat 4. Music: drone and a soft santoor figure; Bhupali.

**Beat 6: The gourd and the golden egg** — picture: sentence 0, an ash gourd drawn in fine gold line on a low stone ledge at dusk, with a Diya beside it (an offering). Sentence 1, the gourd's outline glows softly. Sentence 2, the camera drifts up into the sky above the ledge. Sentence 3, a golden embryo of light (the CosmicEgg, smaller and deeper gold) glows high in the sky. Sentence 4, the egg's light becomes the sun's disc, rising behind the Ridge. Sentence 5, the sun's warm point, the egg-glow and the gourd's glow line up in one vertical column of light; final frame holds. Camera: drift, then crane up, then slow pushIn on the column. Freeze on the final frame. Intensity 3. Palette `goldenEgg`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm; drone, motif (fragment), santoor, resolution; a soft bell on sentence 5. Ambience: an occasional soft evening gust.

**Beat 7: The theme** — backdrop, faint behind the words: the warm point of light in a small open palm of gold rays, like a hand still holding what it made, with a thin thread of light from it to a tiny sun. Music: Yaman, 58 bpm, drone and one bansuri phrase.

**Beat 8: Hagrid's cake at midnight** — letterbox bars throughout. Picture: sentence 0, a small hut on a black rock in a grey sea, rain slanting, one dim window; a few envelopes drift and settle on the waves. Sentence 1, inside: a bare floor in shadow and a faint luminous watch dial counting. Sentence 2, a coat-hanger outline hangs briefly in the dark, then fades. Sentence 3, a sudden bang: the door bursts inward and warm lantern light floods the floor; a huge shadow fills the doorway (no figure, no face). Sentence 4, a small, slightly squashed box in a pool of light. Sentence 5, the lid lifts and a soft glow rises from the cake (no lettering shown). Sentence 6, the light holds; nothing moves. Sentence 7, a fire catches in the grate, sparks rise, and the room turns amber. Camera: aerial over the sea, then pushIn through the window; still from sentence 4 to 6; slow pullBack on sentence 7. Slow motion: sentence 3, 3 s, as the door opens. Freeze after sentence 6. Intensity 4. Palette `stormHut`. Music: hogwarts, 64 bpm; low string drone and low woodwind for sentences 0–2; a soft timpani-like thud (not a cymbal) on sentence 3; celesta and harp from sentence 4; strings warm on sentence 7. Ambience: rain and sea for the opening of the scene (quiet), then occasional gusts; fire crackle (quiet, continuous) from sentence 7.

**Beat 9: Think it through** — backdrop: the hut's fire, glowing low. Music: drone only during the activity, with the timer's soft tick.

**Beat 10: The Christmas jumper** — letterbox bars throughout. Picture: sentence 0, a tall window with soft snow-light, a dark dormitory. Sentence 1, a small pile of parcels at the foot of a bed, catching the light. Sentence 2, a single coin glints, small and cold; then a lumpy parcel. Sentence 3, the parcel opens and a hand-knitted jumper unfolds in knit emerald (stitch texture, no letter on it), beside a box of fudge. Sentence 4, a second, folded jumper, darker, at the next bed. Sentence 5, the two jumpers side by side. Sentence 6, a soft candle-glow warms the scene. Sentence 7, a thin thread of warm light runs from the jumper out of the window towards a far, distant house-light (the warmth comes from somewhere). Camera: drift across the room, then slow pushIn on the jumper; still from sentence 3. Freeze after sentence 3. Intensity 3. Palette `christmasMorning`. Music: hogwarts, 60 bpm; harp and celesta, soft strings; a light bell on sentence 3. Ambience: none.

**Beat 11: Think it through** — backdrop: the jumper by the window, snow falling slowly. Music: drone only during the activity.

**Beat 12: Side by side** — backdrop: faint night sky with the warm point of light and a candle side by side, never merged. Music: Yaman, drone and santoor, 56 bpm.

**Beat 13: Today** — backdrop: faint night sky with a few small diya points. Music: Yaman, drone and soft santoor.

**Beat 14: Reflection, then the closing** — backdrop: the low ridge before dawn, the warm point of light on a stone ledge (mirroring Beat 1). On 14.2, golden dust rises once. The closing (about 13 seconds): the sun rises behind the ridge with the warm point at its core; the theme's word and idea fade in large and hold; the point drifts up as a spark and becomes diya 4 on the map. Music: Yaman, very soft, under the questions; Bhupali for the theme again; at the close the bansuri plays the full motif, a temple bell, then a 5 s tanpura tail. Palette `reflective`, then `himalayanDawn` for the closing.

Musical arc: silence and Malkauns give way to Bhupali at the smile, which blooms again at the sun and then settles; there is no climax and no percussion above a soft tabla. The Hogwarts beats are their own small arc (a storm that opens into firelight, then a quiet morning), and the night closes in Yaman, then Bhupali at sunrise.

## New drawing pieces

- **CosmicEgg:** a glowing ovoid of light with a soft inner gradient and a faint rim; can open into Particles (used in Beats 2, 3, 6).
- **SunDisc:** a sun with a slow-turning corona, heat shimmer and a calm warm point at its centre (Beats 4, 6, closing; reusable for later dawns).
- **EightRays:** eight fine rays opening from a glow like a wheel, each with an anchor for a SymbolIcon: the Devi's arms as light (reusable for other eight-armed forms).
- **New SymbolIcon shapes:** kamandalu, amrita kalasha, bow, arrow, discus (chakra), mace (gada), japa mala, lion, ash gourd, bell (also wanted for Night 3).
- **Devi light silhouette:** a very light fine-line head-and-shoulders outline that can fade in and out within a Glow, with no features (first used here; reusable on any night).
- **Hogwarts objects:** a hut on a rock in a storm, a door that bursts open with a flood of light, a doorway-filling shadow, a small squashed cake box, a hearth fire, a dormitory window with snow-light, a parcel pile, a knitted jumper with stitch texture (no lettering), a fudge box.
