# Night 7 direction: Kalaratri, the dark night

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-7.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "ink, ember and starlight".** The deepest indigo-black night of the nine, lit by ember red and a few cold stars. The Devi's light is warm gold at first, and darkens to a deep indigo glow with an ember core as Kali and then Kalaratri. Demons are grey-brown smoke with dull ember points, never as dark or as clear as the Devi's own darkness, so her dark always reads as light, not shadow. Hogwarts scenes use cooler lamp-amber and silver-blue.
- **Signature motif: the open hand.** A small gold outline of the raised open palm (abhaya mudra):
  - It glows faintly for a moment in the opening, before anything else is drawn.
  - It is hidden through the battles, while the night fills with smoke.
  - It appears in front of Kalaratri's dark light in Beat 8, facing the viewer, and is the first hotspot.
  - It sits behind the theme's words in Beat 9, and returns at the close as the darkness turns to the first light before dawn.
- A second thread is **the drop that never lands**: ember sparks falling towards a ground line. In Beat 5 each one that lands becomes new smoke. In Beat 7 they are caught before they touch it. The theme image uses it once more.
- **How the night ends:** the darkest sky of the series lightens to a pale pearl before dawn (a hint of tomorrow's Mahagauri, never a full sunrise). The open hand fades into a spark, and the map lights diya 7.

**Ragas** (comment each one in the score code; root D, tanpura Sa–Pa as on Night 1)

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Malkauns (new, approved) | Grave, deep-night stillness | Opening, Beat 8, closing (first half) | A late-night raga with no Re or Pa (S g m d n), heavy and inward; the sound of "the dark night" that is calm, not menacing. |
| Marwa | Twilight tension that never settles | Beats 2–7 | No Pa to rest on, so the threat of the army and the multiplying demon never resolves until Beat 7's end. |
| Yaman | Tender, reflective evening | Beats 9, 14, 15, 16 | The warm reflective voice the series uses for ideas and talk. |
| Bhupali | Serene, open, bright | The closing (second half) | The pearl light before dawn: Night 1's raga of serene devotion, as a hint that light returns. |

**Hogwarts scenes (Beats 10–13):** the original palette (celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings, low woodwind), D Dorian, stepwise motifs in a narrow range. Never imitate any film theme; no 3/8 waltz.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: a black frame; the open-hand icon glows faintly for two seconds and fades (sentence 0), then a deep night sky with sparse stars over a low ridge, and a small warm flame in the centre (sentence 1); camera: slow pushIn; intensity 2; palette `darkNight`; music: indian, Malkauns, 54 bpm, drone + motif (bansuri, low register), one temple bell at the start; ambience: a single soft gust.

**Beat 2: The order to seize her** — picture: sentence 0, a dark sky with three smoke shapes crowding out small god-lights (Shiva silver, Vishnu blue, Brahma rose-gold, Indra pale white-gold) which drift off-frame; sentences 1–2, two small smoke shapes see a warm glow on a far peak; sentence 3, a ring of sound spreads from the glow and one smoke shape dissolves; sentence 5, a mass of smoke shapes with ember points advances; sentence 6, the Devi's warm flame seated on a golden peak, a small gold lion icon beside it; final frame (sentence 8), the flame darkens from gold to deep indigo with an ember core; camera: aerial, then lowAngle on the peak; freeze after the last sentence; intensity 5; palette `goldenPeak` changing to `darkNight`; music: indian, Marwa, 66 bpm, drone + motif + percussion (soft low drum from sentence 5); a low swell at the darkening; ambience: soft occasional gusts.

**Beat 3: What will she do with her anger? (choice)** — backdrop: the frozen final frame of Beat 2, breathing gently. Music: Marwa drone only, with a soft tabla pulse.

**Beat 4: From the darkened brow** — picture: sentence 0, a second, deeper glow rises from the Devi's flame: Kali, a dark-light indigo form with an ember core and a very faint fine-line head-and-shoulders outline with loose flowing hair lines, no features; sentence 1, small gold sword and noose icons settle beside her (no skulls are drawn); sentences 2–3, smoke shapes are drawn into her darkness and vanish; sentence 4, two larger smoke shapes rush in; sentence 5, they dissolve into ash-grey drift (no heads, no bodies); final frame, Kali's glow bows towards the Devi's flame, and the flame brightens; camera: lowAngle, then pullBack; slow motion on sentence 5 (3 s); intensity 7; palette `smokeBattle`; music: indian, Marwa, 84 bpm, drone + motif + percussion + strings + climax (low drums, swelling strings) on sentence 5, falling to drone for the naming (sentence 6), one bell on "Chamunda"; parent bell: start of beat, and before sentence 5.

**Beat 5: The demon who multiplied** — picture: sentence 0, smoke armies on the horizon; sentence 1, seven lights rise from the god-lights, each with a small gold mount icon (swan, bull, peacock, Garuda, boar, lion, elephant) in its own soft colour (rose-gold, silver, sapphire, blue, earth amber, saffron, pale white-gold); sentences 3–4, one tall smoke shape steps forward; ember sparks fall from it to the ground line and each landing spark rises as a copy; sentences 5–7, a thunderbolt icon flash and a discus icon arc, each followed by more copies, until the sky is full; final frame, the seven lights small against a sky of copies; camera: pan, then pullBack to a wide; freeze after the last sentence; intensity 8; palette `matrikas` changing to `smokeBattle`; music: indian, Marwa, 92 bpm, all layers except resolution; each landing spark a soft low thud (quiet, not percussive bursts); ambience: none beyond the score.

**Beat 6: Every blow makes more (choice)** — backdrop: the frozen sky full of copies, breathing. Music: Marwa drone with a low, uneasy string pedal.

**Beat 7: Before it touches the ground** — picture: sentence 0, the Devi's flame brightens (the laugh is a ripple of light, no sound caption beyond "a low laugh of bells" if captions are on); sentences 1–2, Kali's darkness widens into a low arc above the ground line; sentences 4–5, sparks fall and are caught in the arc before they reach the ground, each going out softly; sentence 6, the copies thin and the tall smoke shape fades; final frame, the sky clears to stars and the seven lights and god-lights glow; camera: still, then crane up as the sky clears; slow motion on sentence 5 (4 s), on the catching; intensity 9 falling to 5; palette `smokeBattle` changing to `darkNight`; music: indian, Marwa, 96 bpm, climax on the catching, then drop to drone + resolution (singing bowl) as the sky clears; bell when the last copy fades.

**Beat 8: The one who does good** — picture: sentence 0, the Devi's light as Kalaratri: a dark-light indigo glow with an ember core, a faint fine-line head-and-shoulders outline with loose hair lines; sentence 3, a faint line of lightning light curves around it like a garland; sentence 4, three small points of light above the outline, flame wisps at the breath; sentence 5, a small gold donkey icon; sentences 6–7, four small gold icons: curved sword, hook, raised open hand (glowing brightest, facing the viewer) and lowered open hand; final frame, the open hand holds in front of the glow; camera: slow pushIn; freeze after sentence 7; intensity 4; palette `kalaratri`; music: indian, Malkauns, 56 bpm, drone + motif (bansuri, the night's motif heard whole) + resolution (temple bell on the open hand); ambience: none. Symbols step: each icon brightens in turn as its meaning shows. Did-you-know step: a faint blue light (Vishnu) resting in a dark calm sea of night, waking.

**Beat 9: The theme** — a small steady flame in a wide dark night; ember drops fall towards a ground line and fade out before they touch it; the open-hand icon faint behind the words. Music: Yaman, 60 bpm, tanpura and slow santoor, pp.

**Beat 10: The boggart in the wardrobe** — letterbox; picture: sentence 0, a candlelit room and a tall wardrobe that rattles; sentence 1, a swirl of grey smoke spills out; sentence 2, the smoke tumbles into a heap of soft fabric and the candles flare (laughter as light bursts; no figure drawn); sentence 3, the smoke turns towards the viewer, a shadow steps between, and the smoke becomes a pale full moon which pops into mist; sentence 5, two steaming teacups by a window; sentence 7, a full moon over dark trees; camera: drift, pushIn on sentence 3, still for the tea; slow motion on sentence 3 (3 s); freeze on the moon; intensity 4; palette `boggartRoom`; music: hogwarts, 72 bpm, low woodwind and pizzicato, celesta bells on the laughter, a soft harp for the tea; ambience: the wardrobe's rattle (short), a ticking clock.

**Beat 11: Think it through** — backdrop: the wardrobe softly blurred, one candle brightening as each rung opens. Music: hogwarts drone, ducked for the activity.

**Beat 12: The Prince's Tale** — letterbox; picture: sentence 0, a stone basin with silver mist; sentence 1, in the mist, a simple swing on a chain moving gently, then a crack of light across it; sentence 2, a candle going out (Lily's family; no figures); sentence 3, a closed door; sentence 4, the silver doe as a soft silver light that moves like a deer, with no detailed outline; sentence 5, the mist settles; sentence 6, a cold, hard-edged classroom candle-row; final frame, the basin still, one candle; camera: crane down into the basin, drift through the memories, pullBack at the end; freeze after sentence 4; intensity 7; palette `pensieve`; music: hogwarts, 60 bpm, soft strings and low woodwind, harp falling on sentence 2, celesta on the silver light, strings thin and cool for sentence 6; parent bell: start of beat, and before sentence 2. (Captions: sounds only.)

**Beat 12 (gentle alternative): The Grim** — letterbox; picture: sentence 0, a large dark four-legged shadow at the edge of a lamp's light in fog; sentence 1, a tunnel of roots under a twisted tree, leading to a boarded-up room lit by one shaft of moonlight; sentence 2, the shadow rises and dissolves into a tall, dim column of lamplight (no figure is drawn); sentence 3, a raised wand of light, trembling; sentence 4, a second lamp glows in the doorway, and a small scurrying shadow is caught in a ring of light; sentence 5, the wand of light lowers and the two lights draw close and warm; sentence 6, two wands of light rise towards the small shadow, and a third, smaller wand of light moves between them; final frame (sentence 7), the wands lowered, warm light in the boarded room; camera: drift, crane down into the tunnel, slow pushIn in the room; slow motion on sentence 6 (3 s); freeze on the final frame; intensity 4; palette `grimNight`; music: hogwarts, 66 bpm, low woodwind, a tense string pedal for sentences 2–4, harp and celesta warming from sentence 5; ambience: one soft gust, creaking boards (short).

**Beat 13: Think it through** — backdrop: the basin (or the warm lamp for the alternative), blurred. Music: hogwarts drone, ducked for the activity.

**Beat 14: Side by side** — full width over a faint night sky with the open-hand icon. Music: Yaman drone, pp.

**Beat 15: Today** — full width over a faint night sky; small ember drops fading before a ground line. Music: Yaman, tanpura and santoor, pp.

**Beat 16: Reflection, then the closing** — talk step over a faint night sky; theme step with the open hand. Closing (about 13 s): the night lightens to `preDawn` pearl, the theme's word and idea fade in large and hold, the open hand becomes a spark and the map lights diya 7. Music: Malkauns bansuri phrase, then Bhupali *Sa Re Ga Pa* as the sky lightens, one temple bell, tanpura tail.
