# Night 9 direction: Siddhidatri, giver of perfection

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-9.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "lotus, pearl and the tenth dawn".** Deep violet night and pearl-white light belong to Siddhidatri; soft lotus pink and full gold belong to her lotus seat. The riverbank of the two seekers is slate and sand. The night ends in the saffron dawn of Vijayadashami, with a single touch of sindoor red. The Hogwarts scenes keep their own misty white, ink-blue and candle-amber palette, so the two worlds never blur.
- **Signature motif: the lotus of light.** Siddhidatri's full-blown lotus seat is the night's one visual thread. It appears closed at the opening (Night 8's diya on the map becomes a bud), opens petal by petal as she gives the siddhis (Beat 2), holds Ardhanarishvara's light at its centre (Beat 4), floats on the river where the Devi appears (Beats 5 and 7), and in the look-back (Beat 15) becomes the centre of a ring of nine diyas, one lit for each night.
- **How the night ends:** the ring of nine diyas rises into the nine-diya map, every diya lit and the dotted path complete, as the saffron dawn of Vijayadashami comes up behind it. The theme's word and idea fade in over the dawn. A soft conch (her symbol, "a call that announces victory") sounds once, then a temple bell.
- **Musical motif.** Night 1's bansuri phrase in Bhupali, *Sa Re Ga Pa* ("the mountain rising"), returns whole only twice: at the opening, and as the ninth diya lights in the look-back. The falling answer, *Dha Pa Ga Re Sa* ("coming home"), is saved for the very end of the series.
- **Music settings:** root D, tanpura on Sa–Pa (D3, A3) for all traditional beats. Tempo 56–66 BPM throughout; nothing on this night goes above mf. Signature instrument: santoor (the giver's shimmer), with bansuri for the series motif.

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bhupali | Serene devotion, open and bright | Beats 1, 2, 3, 7 and 15 | The series began in Bhupali on Night 1; returning to it for the giver of perfection and the look-back closes the circle. |
| Yaman | Tender evening, warm and reflective | Beats 4, 9, 14, 16 and 17 | Its gentle tivra Ma suits Shiva and Shakti as one being, and the quiet, thoughtful screens. |
| Bageshri | Longing | Beats 5 and 6 | Two men who have lost everything and still worry about the people who wronged them. |
| Bhairav (new) | Dawn, grave and devotional | Beat 8 and the closing | A morning raga for the tenth day's dawn; its komal Re and Dha give a sense of sacred weight without tension. |

Marwa is not used: nothing on this night is tense or unsettled.

- **Hogwarts scenes (Beats 10–13):** the original palette only: celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings, low woodwind, in D Dorian, stepwise and narrow in range. Never imitating any film theme, no 3/8 waltz, no leaping minor-key celesta line.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: Night 8's lit diya on the map shrinks into a closed lotus bud of light on still water under a violet sky; eight faint diyas glow along the far horizon (the nights so far). Step 1.2: the bud breathes. Camera: slow pushIn. Intensity 2; palette `siddhiLotus`; music: indian, Bhupali, 56 bpm, layers drone + motif; cues: temple bell at the start, the bansuri *Sa Re Ga Pa* whole as the bud appears. Ambience: none, or a single soft water lap.

**Beat 2: The giver of perfection** — picture: sentence 0, the lotus opens fully and her warm light settles on it, with a very light-touch silhouette (a faint head-and-shoulders line within the glow, no features); a faint lion outline lies beside the lotus (the other seat). Sentence 1: four small gold outline icons fade in around her: discus, conch, mace, lotus. Sentences 2–3: rings of small figures gather in the dark: sage outlines (topknot), musician outlines, a few gods as small coloured lights, and asuras as soft smoke shapes with ember points, all turned towards her. Sentences 4–7: eight tiny points of light leave the lotus one by one and drift outwards to the gathering; on sentence 7 one point shrinks to a speck and another swells large. Final frame: the lotus at centre, eight sparks hanging in the air. Camera: crane up from the water to a wide view. Freeze after sentence 3 (the gathering). Intensity 3; palette `siddhiLotus`; music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm, drone + motif (santoor) + soft strings; cues: a santoor shimmer for each of the eight sparks. Hotspots (step 2.2): the six symbols as gold icons. Ambience: none.

**Beat 3: Eight gifts (choice)** — backdrop: the eight sparks hover in front of the viewer, breathing. Music: Bhupali drone and a held santoor note; at the reveal, the sparks dim slightly (the warnings).

**Beat 4: Half of Shiva** — picture: step 4.1, sentence 0, Shiva's cool silver light bows before a great warm light; sentence 1, the warm light flows into the silver until one tall oval of light stands on the lotus, its left half silver and its right half warm saffron, joined by a soft seam (formless: no body is drawn, and the light-touch silhouette is not used here). Step 4.2: Brahma, as a god, is a soft rose-gold light; sentence 1, the world around him is bare and still; he meditates (sentence 2), and the one oval appears before him; sentence 3, a thread of warm light leaves the oval and travels to a small outline palace (Daksha's house); sentences 4–5, the warm light returns and merges back; sentences 6–8, the picture cross-fades for a moment to Night 1's single flame on Kailasa, then back. Camera: still, then a slow orbit around the joined light. Freeze after sentence 2 of step 4.1 (one being). Intensity 4; palette `ardhanari`; music: indian, Yaman, 60 bpm, drone + motif + strings; cues: a singing bowl as the two lights join; Night 1's *Sa Re Ga* fragment at the cross-fade. Ambience: none.

**Beat 5: Two seekers by the river** — picture: sentences 1–2, a king's outline (kirita mukuta, dhoti and shawl) rides alone into a dark forest of fine-line trees; sentence 3, a merchant's outline (plain head, shawl) sits under a tree; sentences 4–6, a sage's outline (topknot) sits with them, and as he speaks, tiny scenes from earlier nights glow faintly in the air above (a column of gathered light, a dark smoke shape, a fierce warm light) and fade; sentences 7–8, a pale sandbank by a river, and a small earthen image (a simple clay form, no detail) with a diya before it; sentence 9, seasons pass: flowers, incense smoke and water drops, the two outlines kneeling; sentence 10, the Devi's warm light rises over the water on the lotus, with the light-touch silhouette. Camera: drift through the forest, then a slow pan along the river. Slow motion at sentence 10 (4 s). Intensity 4; palette `riverbank`; music: indian, Bageshri, 58 bpm, drone + motif (bansuri) + light tabla; cues: a temple bell at sentence 10. Ambience: an occasional soft gust in the forest; a gentle, quiet water lap at the river.

**Beat 6: What would you ask for? (choice)** — backdrop: the two kneeling outlines before the Devi's light, held, breathing. Music: Bageshri drone only.

**Beat 7: What the Devi gave** — picture: sentence 0, a small gold crown icon and a palace outline glow above the king; sentences 1–3, the merchant's outline, and inside it a small flame grows quietly; sentence 4, two threads of her light reach both men at once; sentences 5–6, the palace outline brightens and, far off, a line of faint future figures (outlines) recedes into time; sentence 7, the merchant's flame steadies and his outline seems to lighten; sentence 8, the Devi's light gathers into the lotus and vanishes, leaving a soft afterglow; sentence 9, only the earthen image and the diya remain. Camera: pullBack to a wide riverbank. Freeze after sentence 9 for the "Notice" line. Intensity 6 (the night's peak); palette `riverbank` warming to `siddhiLotus`; music: indian, Bhupali, 62 bpm, drone + motif + strings + resolution; cues: the climax layer swells gently at sentence 4 (mf, no more); a single bell at sentence 8. Ambience: water lap.

**Beat 8: The tenth day** — picture: the riverbank at night, the sky beginning to pale. Step 8.1: a small icon for each victory: a light pushing back a smoke shape (the Devi and Mahishasura), a bow outline (Rama), a shami tree outline with a bow hidden in its branches (Arjuna). Step 8.2: a tall dark effigy outline dissolving into rising embers (no faces, no fire around any person); a lit image-boat (a warm glow on a raft) floating down a river, with red sindoor dots blooming like petals. Step 8.3: a tray of rice with a small fingertip line tracing a first letter (no letter is shown); a palace outline traced in tiny lights; a two-lobed apta leaf; small outline palanquins moving along a valley path. Step 8.4: books, a pen, a veena-like instrument and a laptop resting before a diya. Camera: a slow pan across the vignettes, each fading in on its sentence. Intensity 4; palette `vijayaDawn`; music: indian, Bhairav (new), 60 bpm, drone + motif + light percussion; cues: a soft dhak-like drum pattern for Bengal, very quiet; bell at 8.4. Ambience: a little fire crackle for the effigy only.

**Beat 9: The theme** — backdrop, faint behind the words: a calm sea of gold light at night; one small wave rises, glints, and settles back into it, with no line where the wave ends. Music: Yaman drone and a slow santoor phrase, 56 bpm.

**Beat 10: The Elder Wand** — picture (letterbox bars): the beat opens on 3 s of white mist before sentence 0, so the parent bell's second chime has room. Sentence 0, bright mist, the faint arches of a station, a bench, a lamp. Sentences 1–2, a pale flame flickers low, then steadies (the confession). Sentence 3, darkness clears to a grey dawn; a thin wand of light arcs through the air and settles, held in the frame's centre. Sentence 4, a wooden desk in a round, shadowed office; a broken wand of light lies in two pieces; one touch of the bright wand, and the pieces join with a small glow. Sentence 5, a white marble tomb outline by dark water; the bright wand is laid down, and its glow goes quiet. Sentences 6–7, captions for sound only. Final frame: the tomb under a paling sky. Camera: drift through the mist, pushIn on the desk. Slow motion at sentence 4 (3 s, the mending). Freeze after sentence 4. Intensity 5; palette `kingsCross` changing to `greatHall`; music: hogwarts, D Dorian, 60 bpm, low string pad + harp + celesta; cues: one bell at the mending; the pad thins to a single low woodwind note as the wand is laid down. Ambience: none in the mist; a single soft gust by the water. Parent bell: at the start of the beat and again just before sentence 0.

**Beat 11: Think it through** — backdrop: the desk with two wands, held faintly. Music: hogwarts low pad only; activity ducking as in Night 1.

**Beat 12: The Mirror and the Stone** — picture (letterbox bars): sentence 0, a tall mirror frame in a moonlit, empty classroom; two warm glows appear in the glass behind a small candle point (no figures). Sentence 1, the glass clouds over. Sentence 2, a stone chamber with torchlight and the same mirror. Sentence 3, a cold, dark shadow shape stands beside the mirror. Sentence 4, a small ember-red point shows in the glass, out of reach. Sentence 5, the point leaves the glass and drops into the foreground. Sentence 6, the glass clears to soft light. Sentence 7, the red point fades to dust. Camera: slow pushIn on the mirror; still in the chamber. Slow motion at sentence 5 (3 s). Freeze after sentence 5. Intensity 4; palette `mirrorChamber`; music: hogwarts, D Dorian, 58 bpm, harp + celesta + low woodwind; cues: a celesta note as the point drops. Ambience: a faint torch flicker in the chamber.

**Beat 13: Think it through** — backdrop: the mirror frame, glass softly lit. Music: hogwarts pad only.

**Beat 14: Side by side** — backdrop: faint night sky with the lotus of light on one side and a wand of light on the other, far apart. Music: Yaman drone.

**Beat 15: Nine nights, looking back** — picture: step 15.1, the lotus of light at centre on dark water; as each night's theme word and idea appear, one diya around it lights, in order, with that night's small symbol beside it as a gold icon (moon, rosary and water-pot, bell, sun, lotus with child-glow, sword of light, raised hand, bull, lotus). As the ninth lights, the ring turns once, slowly. Step 15.2: the ring holds; fine gold threads link the diyas named in each thread. Camera: crane up to an aerial view of the ring. Freeze after the ninth diya. Intensity 4; palette `siddhiLotus`; music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm, drone + motif + strings; cues: a soft bell as each diya lights (nine, very quiet, slightly different pitches from the raga); the bansuri plays *Sa Re Ga Pa* whole at the ninth. Ambience: none.

**Beat 16: Today** — backdrop: the ring of diyas, faint. Music: Yaman drone and a slow santoor line.

**Beat 17: Reflection, then the closing** — backdrop: the ring of diyas; at 17.2 the sky begins to pale. Music: Yaman, 56 bpm, drone + resolution. Closing: Bhairav at dawn, then the bansuri's falling answer *Dha Pa Ga Re Sa*, a soft conch, one temple bell, and a 5 s tanpura tail to silence.
