# Night 3 direction: Chandraghanta, the moon-bell

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-3.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "moon-silver and bell-gold".** Cool moon-silver for calm, warm bell-gold for readiness, held together on one forehead. The wedding day opens in marigold festival light; Shiva's procession brings smoky violet and ash; Chandraghanta's radiance returns the hall to gold and silver together. The Hogwarts scenes keep their own cooler candle-amber and ink-blue world.
- **Signature motif: the bell-moon and its rings.** A thin crescent that also reads as a bell. It hangs faintly in the sky from the opening, settles on Chandraghanta's forehead in Beat 6, and its sound is drawn as slow concentric rings of light that calm whatever they touch (they never strike anything). Parvati is the warm flame carried over from Nights 1 and 2.
- **How the night ends:** the rings slow and fade, the crescent rests above a single diya, and the map's third diya lights under it.
- **Musical motif:** a short phrase in Raga Durga, *Sa Re Ma Pa* (rising, "the bell lifted") answered by *Dha Pa Ma Re Sa* ("the bell at rest"), heard whole at the opening, in Beat 6 and at the close. Root D, tanpura on Sa–Pa, as on Night 1.
- **The bell sound:** one soft temple bell is the night's signature cue. It always rings *before* anything happens, never on an impact.

**Ragas** (comment each one in the score code)

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bhupali | Serene, open devotion | Opening, Beat 2, Beat 7 reveal | Festive expectation, and the return to joy when the wedding goes ahead. |
| Marwa | Twilight unease with no resting note | Beats 3–5 | Mena's terror and anger: nothing settles until someone calms it. |
| Durga (new, approved) | Bright, steady, devotional valour (S R M P D) | Beats 6 and 8, closing | Traditionally linked with the Devi; strong without being harsh, which is exactly "fierce and calm at once". |
| Yaman | Tender evening, reflective | Beat 9, Beats 14–16 | Warm and thoughtful for the theme and the Reflect section. |
| Bageshri | Longing, gentle ache | Beat 4 | Parvati caught between two people she loves. |

**Hogwarts scenes (Beats 10–13):** the separate original palette: celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings, low woodwind; D Dorian; stepwise motifs in a narrow range. Never imitate any film theme, no 3/8 waltz, no leaping minor celesta line. Molly's duel uses low strings and low woodwind only, with no celebratory swell at its end.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: night sky over the mountain ridge; Parvati's warm flame (from Night 2) burns steadily on a ledge; a thin bell-moon crescent fades in high above. On "Tonight's form", the crescent brightens and one slow ring of light spreads from it. Camera: slow pushIn. Intensity 2. Palette `kailasa`. Music: indian, Durga motif whole, 60 bpm, drone + motif; one bell at the start. Ambience: a soft gust now and then.

**Beat 2: Waiting for the bridegroom** — picture: Himavat's palace as fine gold line-art on the mountain, marigold garlands; Mena as a veiled outline at a balcony, Narada a topknot outline beside her (sentence 0–1). Sentences 3–5: the gods pass in the sky as distinct trails of light: Indra a bright white-gold, the moon pale silver, the sun warm gold, Brihaspati soft amber. Each trail pauses; Narada's outline gives a small shake of the head. Final frame: the last trail fades and the valley path is empty. Camera: pan along the procession. Intensity 3. Palette `weddingDay`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 66 bpm, drone + motif + light percussion (festive tabla); a small bell as each god appears. Ambience: distant crowd murmur in short swells, not a bed.

**Beat 3: Shiva as he is** — picture: sentence 0–1: the sky dims and the ganas arrive as a crowd of wild, flickering shapes of shadow and light with ember points, drums glinting, rushing like wind; never monstrous detail, never demons. Sentence 3: Shiva arrives as a cool silver light, with small gold outline icons of the bull and crescent moon beside it (no skull, hide or body drawn). Sentence 4: Narada's outline points. Sentence 5: Mena's outline sinks to the ground; maids' outlines gather. Camera: lowAngle for the procession, then pullBack. Freeze after sentence 4 ("That is Shiva"). Intensity 6. Palette `procession`. Music: indian, Marwa, 80 bpm, drone + percussion (frame drums, horns as low strings) + strings; drums drop out at Mena's faint. Ambience: soft gusts as the ganas pass.

**Beat 4: What would you do, Parvati?** — backdrop: Beat 3's last frame breathing gently, Parvati's flame between Mena's fallen outline and the silver light. Music: Bageshri, drone + motif, 60 bpm.

**Beat 5: Mena's anger** — picture: Mena's outline rises, arms raised; a cold gust of grey smoke swirls from her towards Parvati's flame (sentences 0–3). The strike is never drawn: at sentence 3 the gust peaks and the sages' outlines step between, arms in a sweep. Sentences 4–6: through it all Parvati's flame stays perfectly still and upright, its glow steady. Sentence 7: gods and sages gather round Mena as small lights and outlines. Final frame: Mena's outline seated, the smoke thinning. Camera: still, then slow pushIn on the flame. Slow motion on sentence 4 (Parvati stays calm), about 3 s. Intensity 6. Palette `procession`. Music: indian, Marwa, 72 bpm, drone + strings; percussion silent; the motif returns softly on Parvati's answer. Parent bell: at the start of the beat and again just before sentence 3.

**Beat 6: The moon-bell** — picture: sentence 1: Parvati's flame swells into a golden glow, with a very light-touch silhouette (head and shoulders outline) inside the light. Sentence 2: ten small gold weapon icons fan out around the glow, a lion icon below, and the bell-moon settles on the forehead of the silhouette. Sentence 4: rings of light spread outward through the hall; where they pass, the ganas' shadows turn to warm points of light (Shiva's loyal attendants). Sentence 5: the glow turns towards Shiva's silver light. Final frame: gold and silver lights side by side, the crescent between them. Camera: crane up as the form appears, then still. Freeze after sentence 3 ("This was Chandraghanta"). Intensity 7. Palette `moonBell`. Music: indian, Durga, 76 bpm, drone + motif + percussion + climax (conch, swelling strings) peaking on sentence 3, then resolution as the rings spread; bell just before sentence 1. Ambience: none.

**Beat 7: Should Shiva change?** — backdrop: gold and silver lights side by side. At the reveal (7.2), Shiva's silver light brightens into a radiant, softer white-gold column; Mena's outline stands still "like a painting", then bows; the palace garlands brighten. Music: Bhupali returns, drone + motif + light percussion, 68 bpm; a bell on the bow.

**Beat 8: The bell that protects** — picture: the glow of Beat 6, now calm, centred against the night sky. Sentence 2: a single bell icon rings; one ring of light spreads. Sentence 3: a faint gentle glow and a faint fierce glow appear either side and merge into one. Sentence 5: a dark smoke shape at the edge thins and drifts away as a ring reaches it (no contact). 8.2: the symbols (bell-moon, bell, weapons, lotus, rosary, water pot, abhaya palm, lion) are small gold outline icons round the light, each a tappable hotspot. 8.3: Indra's white-gold light passes a small bell into the glow. Camera: still, slow drift. Intensity 5. Palette `moonBell`. Music: indian, Durga, 66 bpm, drone + motif + resolution; bell just before sentence 2.

**Beat 9: The theme (Readiness)** — full width; faint behind the words: a bell hanging perfectly still under the crescent, with one slow ring of light spreading from it and fading. Music: Yaman, drone + motif, 60 bpm.

**Beat 10: Neville stands in the way** — letterbox. Picture: a common room at night drawn as a hearth's low embers, an armchair silhouette and an oval doorway of faint light (sentence 3). Three small candle flames move towards the doorway; a fourth, smaller candle slides out and stands in front of it (sentence 3), its flame trembling (sentence 4). Sentence 5: a pale flash; the small candle tips over stiffly, still lit. Sentence 6: the three flames pass and the doorway closes. Sentence 7: floating candles in a dark hall; ten motes of plain white light drop into a clear glass, which brims. Camera: still, then a pan to the hall. Freeze after sentence 4 (fists raised; the activity's freeze point). Intensity 4. Palette `commonRoomNight`, then `greatHall`. Music: hogwarts, 64 bpm, drone + motif (celesta, harp); low woodwind on the tipping candle; soft strings for the ten points. Ambience: occasional fire crackle.

**Beat 11: Think it through** — backdrop: the doorway of faint light and the small candle standing before it. Music: hogwarts drone only.

**Beat 12: Molly's duel** — letterbox. Picture: a great hall in drifting smoke, broken light through high windows. Sentence 1: a cold, dark light duels three small lights. Sentence 1 end: a pale streak passes just beside one of them. Sentence 2: a warm hearth-like glow, threaded with a fine line of yarn-gold, turns. Sentence 3: it crosses the hall, shedding a dark cloak-shape, and the three small lights draw back. Sentence 4: the dark light flares mockingly. Sentence 6: warm and cold lights exchange fast flashes. Sentence 7: the cold light flickers and dissolves into smoke; no figure falls, nothing is shown hit. Final frame: smoke settling, the warm glow alone and dimmer. Camera: drift, then still. Slow motion on the near miss (sentence 1), about 2 s. Intensity 7. Palette `battleHall`. Music: hogwarts, 84 bpm, drone (low strings) + percussion (low drum) + strings; everything drops to the drone at sentence 7, no climax or resolution. Captions: sounds only (no shouted line). Parent bell: at the start and again just before sentence 7.

**Beat 12 (gentle alternative): Harry saves Dudley** — letterbox. Picture: a summer alley at dusk under sodium streetlamps, heat haze. Sentence 0: two small lights walking, one larger. Sentence 1: frost creeps across the ground, the lamps go out, two tall shadow shapes glide in. Sentence 3: a shadow closes on the larger light, and a burst of silver light appears. Sentence 4: the silver light runs as a soft, undetailed shape and drives both shadows back; the two lights move on together, one leaning on the other. Sentence 5: a letter of light arrives through the dark. Camera: still, then a short pushIn on the silver burst (sentence 3). Freeze after sentence 2 (the rule he knows). Intensity 5. Palette `summerAlley`. Music: hogwarts, 72 bpm, drone + strings; harp and celesta on the silver light; one low woodwind note on the letter.

**Beat 13: Think it through** — backdrop: smoke settling (or the dark alley with the streetlamps relit, after the alternative). Music: hogwarts drone only.

**Beat 14: Side by side** — full width over a faint night sky with the bell-moon. Music: Yaman drone.

**Beat 15: Today** — full width over a faint night sky. Music: Yaman, drone + soft motif fragments.

**Beat 16: Reflection** — full width; at 16.2, the crescent rests above a single diya. Music: Yaman, then the Durga motif's falling answer and one bell into the closing.

## New drawing pieces

- **Bell-moon:** a thin crescent that also reads as a bell; used in the sky, on Chandraghanta's forehead, behind the theme and in the closing.
- **Sound rings:** slow concentric rings of light from a point; they calm or dissolve what they reach and never strike.
- **SymbolIcon additions:** bell, sword, bow and arrow, mace, rosary, water pot, abhaya palm, lion (trident, lotus, moon and bull already exist).
- **Devi light-touch silhouette:** a faint head-and-shoulders outline inside a glow (new rule, 18 October); reusable on later nights.
- **God trails:** distinct coloured trails of light for a passing procession of gods (Indra, moon, sun, Brihaspati).
- **Gana crowd:** wild flickering shapes of shadow and ember light that can turn into warm friendly points; must not read as demons.
- **Smoke gust:** a cold swirl of grey smoke for anger, which thins and drifts.
- **Hogwarts kit:** small candle figures (standing, tipping), an oval doorway of light, a clear points glass, shadow shapes (dementor-like, undetailed), a running silver light, a letter of light, drifting battle smoke.
