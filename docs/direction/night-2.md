# Night 2 direction: Brahmacharini, the one who practises

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026; Malkauns and the treatment of Shiva's disguise approved by Supriya, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-2.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "white, fire and seasons".** The night begins in Night 1's moonlit Kailasa silver and indigo. Kama's spring is the night's one sweet, false colour (blossom pink and young green), and it ends in ash grey. The tapas turns through the seasons: summer fire orange, monsoon slate, winter ice blue, always around one still white-gold light. It ends on Brahmacharini's white and soft saffron. The Hogwarts scenes keep their own cooler candle-amber, slate and silver-white, so the two worlds never blur.
- **Signature motif: a ring of beads of light (the japa mala).** A faint circle of small gold beads sits low in the frame through the traditional beats. One bead lights at each moment of practice: when Parvati begins serving (Beat 2), when she takes Narada's mantra (Beat 4 reveal), at each season of her tapas (Beat 6), and at each test passed (Beat 7, Beat 8 reveal). In Beat 9 the ring is complete and becomes the rosary in her right hand (a hotspot). At the close, the ring draws in to one bead of light, which becomes diya 2 on the map.
- **Parvati** is a warm flame (Night 1's signature flame, continuing). From Beat 6 a very light-touch silhouette may show within her light: a faint fine-line outline of head and shoulders, no features. **Shiva** is cool silver light. **Kama** is a soft rose light, **Rati** a paler coral light beside him, **Indra** a pale gold-white light. Spring is not a figure: it is blossom and birdsong.
- **How the night ends visually:** Brahmacharini's light walking on bare ground towards Kailasa at dawn, the bead ring complete; then the closing (below).

**Musical motif.** The bansuri plays Night 1's rising *Sa Re Ga Pa* (D E F♯ A), now repeated three times, each a little longer: practice. The answer falls *Dha Pa Ga Re Sa* only at Shiva's reveal (Beat 8) and at the close. Root D, tanpura on Sa–Pa (D3, A3), as Night 1.

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bhupali | Serene devotion | Opening, Beat 2, Beat 8 reveal, Beat 9, closing | The mountain raga of Night 1; Parvati's steadiness and the return of Shiva. |
| Marwa | Twilight unease | Beat 3, Beat 7 | The out-of-season spring is a scheme, and the tests unsettle; no Pa to rest on. |
| Bageshri | Longing | Beat 4, Beat 5 | Parvati's heartbreak, and Mena's loving fear for her daughter. |
| Malkauns (new) | Deep night, inward and austere | Beat 6 | Traditionally a late-night, meditative raga often linked with Shiva; it suits the long, silent tapas. Notes on D: S g m d n (no Re, no Pa). |
| Yaman | Tender evening, reflective | Beats 10, 15, 16, 17 | Warm and thoughtful, as on Night 1, for the theme and Reflect. |

- **Hogwarts scenes (Beats 11–14):** an original palette of celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings and low woodwind, in D Dorian. Motifs move stepwise in a narrow range. No 3/8 waltz, no leaping minor celesta line, never imitating any film theme.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: Night 1's last frame, Shiva's silver light still on the high ridge, Parvati's warm flame far below on the mountain (sentence 0 of "Last night"); the bead ring fades in, unlit. Camera: slow pullBack. Intensity 2; palette `kailasa`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm; drone, motif (the rising phrase once). Ambience: one soft gust.

**Beat 2: The yogi who would not wake** — picture: the high ridge, Shiva's silver light still at the peak with outline attendants on guard (s0). Himavat (crowned outline) climbs with Parvati's flame (s1–2). Shiva's light dims and turns away (s3–4). Parvati's flame brightens as she answers (s5–7). Shiva's light steadies; the flame settles beside it (s8). Final frame: small offerings of flowers and a water pot (gold outline icons) appear and fade, day after day, while the silver light stays still (s9); the first bead lights. Camera: aerial up the ridge, then still. Freeze after s7. Intensity 3; `kailasa`. Music: Bhupali, 62 bpm; drone, motif, light strings. Bell at s9. Ambience: soft occasional gusts.

**Beat 3: Kama's arrow** — picture: Taraka as a far-off dark smoke shape on the horizon with ember points (s1), fading at s2. Indra's pale gold light calls Kama's rose light and Rati's coral light (s3–4). Blossom bursts on bare branches, birds as tiny gold strokes (s5). Parvati's flame approaches; a single fine arrow of light leaves Kama's light (s6). Shiva's silver flickers towards her (s7), then hardens (s8–9). A bright point opens in the silver light, a flash, and Kama's rose light scatters into drifting ash and falling petals (s10). No burning figure. Rati's coral light sinks low (s11); the gods' lights bow (s12); a faint rose ember is kept alive, promised (s13). Shiva's light vanishes (s14); Parvati's flame hurries down the slope (s15). Camera: slow pushIn, a hard cut to still at the flash. Slow motion: s6–s7, about 4 s (the arrow in flight). Freeze after s10. Intensity 6 (7 at the flash); palette `vasanta` to `grief`. Music: Marwa, 72 bpm rising to 84; drone, motif, percussion, strings; climax hit (low drum, no conch) at s10, then silence for 2 s and drone only. Ambience: birdsong in s5–s9 only, then quiet.

**Beat 4: What should Parvati do now?** — backdrop: the empty ridge in ash, Parvati's flame small and low. Reveal: Narada as a topknot outline with a small veena icon; one bead lights as he gives the mantra. Intensity 3; `grief`. Music: Bageshri, 58 bpm; drone and soft bansuri; resolution bell at the reveal.

**Beat 5: "Oh, don't"** — picture: Himavat's lamplit home; Parvati's flame before two outlines, Himavat (crown) and Mena (veiled) (s0). Mena's outline reaches towards her (arms raised, s1–4). The flame holds steady (s5); Mena's arms lower to bless. Final frame: the name *Uma* drawn in fine gold light, then fading (s6–8). Camera: still, then gentle pushIn on s5. Intensity 3; `himavatHome`. Music: Bageshri, 60 bpm; drone, motif, soft strings (sarangi-like). Ambience: a quiet lamp crackle.

**Beat 6: Aparna, the one without leaves** — picture: a bare peak; Parvati's flame with a faint head-and-shoulders outline within it (s0). The season ring turns: fruit icon (s1), leaf icon (s2), the leaf drifts away (s3; bead). The flame very still, a single upright line beneath it (s4). Summer: four small fires around her, the flame unmoved (s5; bead). Monsoon: slate rain lines on rock (s6; bead). Winter: ice-blue water at her base (s7; bead). Saplings outlined, watered (s8). Outline animals lie down in pairs: lion and cow, cat and mouse (s9). Trees fruit and flowers open (s10). Final frame: stars wheel quickly overhead, years passing, the flame unchanged (s11). Camera: orbit, very slow, once around the peak. Freeze after s11. Intensity 4; `tapasSeasons`. Music: Malkauns (new), 54 bpm; drone and motif only, a soft singing bowl at each season change. Ambience: a short fire crackle in summer, quiet rain in the monsoon (both short), nothing in winter. **Parent bell:** start of the beat and before s1.

**Beat 7: Two tests** — picture: the gods' lights feel the heat: a shimmer rises from the peak (s0–1). Seven topknot outlines arrive in a row (s2), speaking (s3–5); her flame flares bright as she laughs (s6–8); the sages bow and leave (s9; bead). An old ascetic outline arrives with umbrella and staff icons, a faint silver edge to his line, the only hint (s10). Parvati's flame bows in welcome (s11); Vijaya, a veiled outline, beside her (s12). The ascetic's gestures sweep (s13–17); the flame does not move. Final frame: a jewel icon and a shard of glass, side by side (s18). Camera: pan between the sages and the flame, then lowAngle on the ascetic. Freeze after s18. Intensity 5; `hermitage`. Music: Marwa, 66 bpm; drone, motif, light percussion, strings; one tabla figure on s8. Ambience: soft gusts.

**Beat 8: How should Parvati answer the stranger?** — backdrop: the frozen last frame of Beat 7. Reveal: her flame rises and turns away; the ascetic's outline dissolves upward into Shiva's silver light, which bows to her flame; the two lights stand together and a bead lights. Intensity 6 at the reveal; `hermitage` to `kailasa`. Music: silence, then Bhupali returns, 60 bpm; the falling answer *Dha Pa Ga Re Sa*, temple bell at the reveal, resolution layer.

**Beat 9: The one who practises** — picture: Brahmacharini as a tall white-gold light, a light-touch head-and-shoulders outline within it (s0); bare footprints of light appear on the ground (s1). The bead ring rises and becomes the rosary icon at her right; a water pot icon at her left (s2). No mount; the ground path stretches ahead (s3). Final frame: her light, the five hotspot icons (rosary, water pot, white cloth, bare feet, calm face as a simple crescent of light). Camera: crane up, then still. Intensity 4; `brahmacharini`. Music: Bhupali, 60 bpm; drone, motif (the rising phrase three times), santoor, resolution bell.

**Beat 10: The theme (Resolve)** — single image faintly behind the words: one small flame, and around it a ring of beads lighting one by one, very slowly. Music: Yaman, 56 bpm; drone and soft bansuri.

**Beat 11: The Patronus lessons** — letterbox. Picture: an empty classroom by candlelight; a tall wardrobe shape (s0). The candles gutter and frost creeps (s1). A thin silver mist from a wand of light, then darkness (s2, twice). A brighter mist holds the cold back for a moment (s3). Candles burn down and are replaced, week after week (s4). The lake at night, ringed by many dark shapes; a blaze of silver from across the water (s5). An hourglass icon turns (s6); the far shore, empty. Final frame: a silver stag of light runs across the lake (s7). Camera: still, then aerial over the lake. Slow motion: s7, about 3 s. Freeze after s7. Intensity 5 (6 at s5); `lessonRoom` to `nightLake`. Music: hogwarts, D Dorian, 66 bpm; low string drone, harp, celesta; strings swell at s7.

**Beat 12: Think it through** — backdrop: faint lake and silver mist. Music: hogwarts drone only (activity rule: −12 dB, a wooden tick each 10 s).

**Beat 13: Occlumency** — letterbox. Picture: a long dark corridor ending in a door, glowing faint blue (s0). A single desk lamp in a stone room (s1). A notebook of nights, pages left blank (s2). A basin of silver light, a ripple, the lamp put out (s3). An exam hall of empty desks; the corridor flashes over it (s4). A small fire in a grate (amber, not green), a brief cold flare (s5). The door opens on darkness; a single candle goes out (s6). Final frame: an office window at dawn, grey and still (s7). No figures, no veil. Camera: slow pushIn down the corridor, then still. Freeze after s6. Intensity 6; `closedDoor`. Music: hogwarts, D Dorian, 60 bpm; low woodwind and string drone; one low pulse at s6, then silence for 2 s. **Parent bell:** start of the beat and before s4.

**Beat 13 (gentle alternative): The Summoning Charm** — letterbox. Picture: a far-off ember-dark smoke shape, the dragon as shadow only (s0); a single lamp (s1); a small broom icon far away, high in a tower window (s2). In an empty classroom, small objects of light twitch and fall back (s3–4). A clock face moves on, lamps burn low, then an object flies cleanly to the hand of light (s5). The arena: the smoke shape coils; a wand of light rises (s6). Final frame: the broom icon streaks in, rises, and a small golden egg glints (s7). Camera: still, then pullBack to the sky. Intensity 3; `midnightPractice`. Music: hogwarts, D Dorian, 72 bpm; celesta, harp, light pizzicato; a bright harp run at s5 and s7.

**Beat 14: Think it through** — backdrop: the closed door, faint (or a lamplit classroom with the gentle alternative). Music: hogwarts drone only (activity rule).

**Beat 15: Side by side** — backdrop: night sky with a faint flame and a faint silver mist side by side. Music: Yaman, 56 bpm; drone, sparse santoor.

**Beat 16: Today** — backdrop: night sky and flame, full width. Music: Yaman drone.

**Beat 17: Reflection** — backdrop: dawn beginning over the ridge; the bead ring complete. Music: Yaman, then the closing in Bhupali: the falling answer, one temple bell, a 5 s tanpura tail.

## New drawing pieces

- **BeadRing:** a circle of small gold beads that light one at a time; becomes the rosary icon. Reusable for any night's counting or practice.
- **SymbolIcon additions:** rosary (japa mala), water pot (kamandalu), white cloth, bare footprint, umbrella and staff, veena, jewel and glass shard, fruit and leaf.
- **DivineLight colours:** rose (Kama), coral (Rati), pale gold-white (Indra), alongside warm, silver and blue.
- **ArrowOfLight:** a fine arrow streak with a soft trail.
- **ThirdEyeFlash:** a bright point opening in a light, with a white flash and fade.
- **SmokeShape:** a dark drifting shadow with ember points, for demons (Taraka here; needed again on later nights).
- **Ash and petals:** an ash variant of Particles that turns into falling petals.
- **SeasonCycle:** small fires ring, rain lines, ice-blue water band, all short and quiet.
- **Outline animals:** lion, cow, cat, mouse, lying down; small birds as strokes.
- **Outline saplings and fruiting trees.**
- **Hogwarts objects:** wardrobe shape, silver mist, silver stag of light, hourglass, corridor with door, basin of light, small broom, clock face.
- **Name glyph:** a fine gold word drawn in light (for *Uma*).
