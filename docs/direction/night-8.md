# Night 8 direction: Mahagauri, the great bright one

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-8.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "sea, seasons and moonlight".** A quiet coast at Prabhasa under a pale moon, the same small grove through four seasons, and a soft pearl-white light that grows slowly. The night ends in a pale dawn over the sea. The Hogwarts scenes use their own cooler candle-amber and ink-blue palettes, so the two worlds never blur.
- **The colour guardrail (most important tonight).** The Devi's light never changes colour from dark to fair. She is the same warm flame from the first beat to the last; what grows in Beat 5 is the *reach and steadiness* of her glow (a wider, calmer halo), not its colour. Nothing on screen links brightness with skin. In Beat 7, the "dark" and "bright" tellings are shown as two equal lights, side by side, neither larger.
- **Signature motif: one lamp and its reflection in still water.** A single diya on the shore at the opening. Its reflection trembles when the joke stings (Beat 2), lies still through the tapas (Beat 4), and widens into a calm ring of light at Gauri (Beat 5). At the close the reflection becomes the eighth diya on the map.
- **How the night ends visually:** the moon fades into a pale dawn over the sea at Prabhasa; the lamp's reflection draws in to a single spark, which becomes diya 8 on the map, beside diya 7 (Kalaratri) glowing just as brightly.
- **Musical motif:** the bansuri's Bhupali "coming home" phrase from Night 1 (*Dha Pa Ga Re Sa*), played slowly, at Gauri (Beat 5) and at the close. Elsewhere only fragments.

**Ragas** (comment each choice in the score code)

| Bageshri | Longing, a quiet hurt | Beats 2–4 | The sting of the joke and the long going-away; its komal Ga and Ni ache without anger. |
| Bhupali | Serene devotion, open and bright | Opening, Beats 5, 8 and the close | The calm brightness of Gauri and Mahagauri; it links back to Shailaputri's bull on Night 1. |
| Yaman | Tender evening, reflective | Beats 6, 9, 14–16 | Warm and thoughtful for the wishes, the theme and the talk at the end. |

Beat 7 (Other tellings) stays on the tanpura drone with soft santoor, in Yaman, so no telling sounds more "right" than another.

**Hogwarts scenes (Beats 10–13):** the original palette of celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings and low woodwind, stepwise motifs in a narrow range, never imitating any film theme.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: night sea at Prabhasa, a single diya on a stone at the water's edge, its reflection long on the water. Sentence 0 (Kalaratri): a faint deep-indigo glow with the small abhaya hand icon fades in at the top of the sky, then softens. Sentence 1 onwards: the moon rises, the lamp brightens; final frame the lamp and its reflection, still. Camera: slow pushIn (30 s). Intensity 2. Palette `prabhasaCoast`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm, drone + motif (fragment); temple bell at frame 0. Ambience: soft waves now and then, no continuous bed.

**Beat 2: A name that stung** — picture: a pleasant place in the mountains at evening: Shiva as a cool silver light and the Devi as a warm flame, side by side. Sentence 2 ("Kali"): Shiva's light flickers lightly, playful. Sentence 4: the Devi's flame dips and her reflection in a nearby pool trembles. Sentence 5: her flame leans away and flares sharply once. Freeze after sentence 4. Camera: still, then drift. Intensity 4. Palette `kailasa`. Music: indian, Bageshri, 60 bpm, drone + motif; a single low santoor note on sentence 4. Ambience: a soft gust. Parent bell at the start and before sentence 2.

**Beat 3: What would you do?** — backdrop: the last frame of Beat 2, breathing. Reveal: her flame moves away down a path towards a distant moonlit coast, small companion lights following. Music: Bageshri, drone only under the cards.

**Beat 4: The grove of tapas** — picture: the same small grove and a linga drawn as a simple gold outline stone, the Devi's flame beside it, always still and at a distance. Sentence 2: the flame stands narrow and upright. Sentence 3: five small fires ring it under a blazing sun. Sentence 4: soft rain lines. Sentence 5: the flame stands in still water, its reflection unbroken. Sentence 6: the four seasons cycle once more quickly behind the unmoving flame. Slow motion at sentence 6 (6 s). Camera: still, then slow pullBack. Intensity 3. Palette `tapasSeasons`. Music: indian, Bageshri, 52 bpm, drone + strings (sarangi-like, sustained); a soft bell at each season change. Ambience: fire crackle (sentence 3, quiet), soft rain (sentence 4), water (sentence 5).

**Beat 5: Gauri** — picture: the grove at night. Sentences 0–1: the flame's glow widens into a calm ring of pearl light over the water; the flame's own colour does not change. Very light-touch silhouette: at sentence 1 a faint fine-line outline of a face and shoulders within the light, for a few seconds only. Sentence 2: Shiva's silver light descends. Sentence 3: his light pulses warmly twice ("Gauri, Gauri"). Sentence 4: a small gold lotus icon (the boon) appears between them. Freeze after sentence 1. Camera: slow pushIn. Intensity 5. Palette `gauriRadiance`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm, drone + motif + resolution; the "coming home" phrase on sentence 1; temple bell on sentence 2. Ambience: quiet waves.

**Beat 6: What would you ask for?** — backdrop: the two lights by the grove. Reveal: small lights of visitors gather along the shore, then a gold outline shrine takes shape; Shiva's light settles into it. Music: Yaman, drone + soft santoor.

**Beat 7: Other tellings** — picture: four small panels of light, one per telling, on a night sky. Kalika Purana: a falling ribbon of light (the heavenly Ganga) over a flame. Shiva Purana: a flame from which a second light separates. Popular telling: river water poured over a flame. Devi Mahatmya: a bright light rising out of a flame that deepens to indigo. At "What the tellings share", the panels draw together into two equal lights, one deep indigo and one pearl, side by side and the same size. Camera: pan across, then still. Intensity 2. Palette `reflective`. Music: indian, Yaman, 56 bpm, drone + santoor only.

**Beat 8: Mahagauri** — picture: the sea at night, a white bull in fine gold line-art resting on the shore; the Devi as a calm warm light above it, with a light-touch outline of head and shoulders (arms are not drawn). Sentence 1: her symbols appear around the light as small gold outline icons: white cloth, bull, trident, damaru, abhaya hand, varada hand (the hotspots). Final frame: light, bull and icons, still. Camera: crane up slowly. Intensity 4. Palette `gauriRadiance`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm, drone + motif + resolution; temple bell on sentence 0. Did you know: small kneeling outline girls with garlands, faint, around a diya. Ambience: waves.

**Beat 9: The theme** — backdrop, faint behind the words: a single diya on the shore, its reflection on still water slowly widening into a calm ring of light. Music: Yaman, drone + slow santoor, pp.

**Beat 10: Kreacher's tale** — letterbox Stage. Picture, told through objects: sentence 0, a dark, cluttered kitchen lit by one candle, a tarnished oval locket on the table. Sentence 2: the scene dims to dark water in a cave, a small boat-shaped glow, a basin with a faint glow (no figures). Sentence 3: a second, smaller candle goes out over the water (Regulus), gently. Sentence 5: the locket is set down beside the candle; the candle steadies. Sentence 6: the kitchen brightens: pans glint, a folded white cloth, the locket gleaming. Sentence 7: many tiny lights stream out of a kitchen door into a dark hall. No characters drawn. Camera: drift, then pushIn on the locket. Freeze after sentence 5. Intensity 5. Palette `grimmauldKitchen`. Music: hogwarts, D Dorian, 60 bpm, low woodwind + soft strings; celesta enters at sentence 5; harp at sentence 6. Ambience: a soft drip in the cave (sentence 2). Parent bell at the start and before sentence 2.

**Beat 11: Think it through** — backdrop: the bright kitchen candle and locket, faint. Music: hogwarts drone, ducked to −12 dB during the activity.

**Beat 12: The silver doe** — letterbox Stage. Sentence 0: an empty tent shape glowing faintly amber in snow, one of three small lights moving away. Sentence 1: bare trees in snow; a soft silver light that moves like a deer (no detailed outline) walks between them; a frozen pool with a sword of light beneath the ice. Sentence 2: the ice breaks; the water darkens and a locket's chain of light tightens (no body). Sentence 3: a warm light rises from the water with the sword. Sentences 4–5: the locket opens into whirling dark smoke; the sword of light comes down and the smoke scatters. Sentences 6–7: the tent glows again with three lights, one standing a little apart. Slow motion at sentence 5 (4 s). Freeze after sentence 3. Camera: lowAngle on the pool, then pullBack. Intensity 6. Palette `forestOfDean`. Music: hogwarts, 64 bpm, harp + celesta (sentence 1), low strings swell to the climax layer at sentence 5 (mf only), resolution on sentence 7. Ambience: a soft gust, ice cracking once (sentence 2). Parent bell at the start and before sentence 2.

**Beat 13: Think it through** — backdrop: the tent in snow, three lights. Music: hogwarts drone, ducked during the activity.

**Beat 14: Side by side** — backdrop: the shore lamp on one side and a candle on the other, at the same height, faint. Music: Yaman drone.

**Beat 15: Today** — backdrop: faint night sky over the shore. Music: Yaman, drone + soft santoor.

**Beat 16: Reflection, then the closing** — backdrop: the shore lamp and its reflection, mirroring Beat 1; golden dust rises once at the theme again. Closing (about 13 s): the moon fades into a pale dawn over the sea (`seaDawn`), *Renewal* and its idea fade in large and hold; the reflection draws in to a spark that becomes diya 8 on the map. Music: Yaman under the questions, Bhupali at the theme, then the bansuri "coming home" phrase in Bhairav, a temple bell and a 5 s tanpura tail.

## New drawing pieces

- **Water reflection** under any light (trembling, still, widening ring): reusable for every shore and pool.
- **Linga:** a simple gold outline stone, small and respectful.
- **Season cycle:** a quick sun, rain, cold-water, sun overlay for one place (useful for Night 2's tapas too).
- **Five fires:** a ring of small fires around a flame.
- **Damaru, abhaya hand and varada hand** as SymbolIcons (the abhaya hand may already be needed for Night 7).
- **Folded white cloth** SymbolIcon (Mahagauri's white dress).
- **Light-touch Devi outline:** a faint fine-line face and shoulders inside a Glow (the 18 October rule), shared by all nights.
- **Hogwarts objects:** locket (plain oval, no emblem), sword of light, deer-like silver light (no outline), tent, cave basin and dark water, kitchen pans.
- **Smoke burst:** dark smoke that scatters when struck by light (shared with demon scenes).
