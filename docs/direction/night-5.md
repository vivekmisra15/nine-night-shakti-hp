# Night 5 direction: Skandamata, mother of Skanda

Status: draft for approval, 18 October 2026. Follows the Visuals rules in CLAUDE.md and the Night 1 revision (docs/direction/night-1.md). Beat numbers match docs/nights/night-5.md.

## Identity

- **Colour identity: "ember, river and lap of gold".** Ember red-orange for Agni and the spark, cool river silver-teal and reed green for the Ganga and the reed forest, pale starlight for the six Krittikas, and a deep warm gold for the Devi's lap of light. Taraka is the night's one dark colour: smoke grey with ember points. The night ends in a soft gold dawn.
- **Signature motif: the spark that is passed on.** One small bright spark carries the night. It falls from Shiva's silver light (Beat 3), is carried by Agni's ember light, the river and the reeds, is ringed by six stars (Beat 4), settles on the Devi's lap of light (Beat 6), leaves it as the bright point of a spear of light (Beat 7) and comes back (Beat 7, end). At the close it rises from her lap, facing outward, and becomes diya 5 on the map. Nobody keeps the spark; everyone carries it for a while, which is the theme.
- **How the night ends visually:** dawn over a low lotus lake; the Devi's warm glow with the small child-light on her lap, both turned towards the horizon. The theme's word and idea fade in over it. The spark lifts off and flies to the map.
- **Musical motif:** a short rising bansuri phrase in Yaman (Ni Re Ga, Ma Ga Re Sa), "held, then sent", heard whole at Beat 6, at Beat 8 and at the close. Fragments elsewhere.

| Raga | Mood | Used in | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marwa | Twilight unease with no Pa to rest on | Beat 2, Beat 7 (battle) | Taraka's rule and the battle never settle until he falls. |
| Bhupali | Serene devotion, open and bright | Opening, Beat 3 (the birth), Beat 8 | The joy of the three worlds at the birth; Skandamata worshipped. |
| Bageshri | Longing | Beats 4–5 | The Krittikas' fierce love, and the fear of losing him at the door. |
| Yaman | Tender evening, warm | Beats 6, 9, 15, 16, closing | A mother's lap; the theme; the night ends warm. |

No new ragas are proposed.

- **Hogwarts scenes (Beats 10–13):** the original palette only: celesta-like bells, harp, soft strings and low woodwind, D Dorian, stepwise motifs in a narrow range. Never imitate any film theme; no 3/8 waltz, no leaping minor celesta line.

## Beats

**Beat 1: Before we begin** — picture: night sky over Kailasa's ridge; last night's warm egg of light (Kushmanda) fades to a single small spark resting in a warm gold glow. Camera drift. Intensity 2. Palette `kailasa`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm; drone, motif (the Yaman phrase previewed once, softly); resolution bell at the start. Ambience: one soft gust.

**Beat 2: A demon with a clever boon** — picture: sentence 0, a dark smoke shape seated in austerity, ember points glowing; sentence 1, Brahma as a soft gold-white light above it. Sentences 2–3, two small gold boon-glyphs pass into the smoke. Sentence 7 onwards, the smoke spreads across three tiers of sky; small outline gods (no faces) are pushed out of their places; an outline elephant icon is drawn into the smoke. Final frame: the gods' outlines gathered before Brahma's light. Camera pullBack then lowAngle. Freeze after sentence 5 ("hardly likely to have a son"). Intensity 5. Palette `tarakaShadow`. Music: indian, Marwa, 66 bpm; drone, motif, low percussion; low drum on sentence 7. Ambience: a low wind gust under the spreading smoke, once.

**Beat 3: A spark too hot to hold** — picture: sentence 0, Shiva's silver light and Parvati's warm flame on Kailasa; sentence 1, small outline gods approach; sentence 2, a single bright spark falls (slow motion, 2.5 s). Sentence 3, a dove of ember-red light (Agni) takes it and glows hotter. Sentences 4–5, the spark passes along a chain: six faint outline women in saris, the mountain ridge, then into a river of light. Sentence 6, the river lays it among tall reeds. Sentence 7, the reeds part on a radiant small light. Final frame: the three worlds' sky brightens; far off on Kailasa, the warm flame brightens too. Camera crane down from peak to river. Intensity 4. Palette `emberRiver`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 62 bpm; drone, motif, strings; santoor shimmer and bell at sentence 7. Ambience: fire crackle for the dove (quiet), then water. Parent bell at the start and before sentence 2.

**Beat 4: Six mothers by the river** — picture: six outline women in saris at the riverbank; above them six stars of the Pleiades, linked by fine lines. Sentence 1, they reach towards the light; sentence 3, six small points of light open around the child-light (the six faces), one turned to each woman. Sentence 4 onwards, a small outline home under the six stars. Camera pushIn. Freeze after sentence 3. Intensity 3. Palette `krittikaStars`. Music: indian, Bageshri, 60 bpm; drone, motif, strings. Ambience: water, soft.

**Beat 5: The messengers at the door** — backdrop: the outline home under six stars, ringed by a wide crowd of outline attendants led by Nandin (outline, no detailed face). On the reveal, the child-light and the six women's outlines travel up the mountain path together. Intensity 4. Palette `krittikaStars`. Music: Bageshri, drone only while choosing; motif returns on the reveal.

**Beat 6: On his mother's lap** — picture: sentence 0, Shiva's silver light and the Devi's warm glow come out on Kailasa; within her glow a very faint fine-line head-and-shoulders outline. Sentence 1, the child-light settles in her lap. Sentence 3, it moves to Shiva's light; a small gold snake icon at his throat wiggles; a soft laugh is shown only as the silver light brightening. Sentence 4 onwards, small gold outline gifts arrive one by one: discus (blue light, Vishnu), trident (silver), thunderbolt and elephant (pale violet light, Indra), spear (ember, Agni). Final frame: the child-light on the lap with the spear icon beside it. Camera still, then slow orbit. Freeze after the last sentence. Intensity 3. Palette `motherGold`. Music: indian, Yaman, 58 bpm; drone, motif (the phrase whole), strings, resolution. Bell on each gift, softly. Ambience: one gust.

**Beat 7: Should he lead the army?** — backdrop: the lap of light and the spear icon. On the reveal: the child-light leaves the lap and becomes the bright point of a spear of light at the head of a line of small outline gods; Taraka is a massing smoke shape; Brahma's gold-white light speaks (shown as a brightening, no words); the spear of light goes in, the smoke dissolves into ember points that go out (slow motion, 2 s); dawn light; the child-light returns to the two laps. No body, no wound. Intensity 7 at the strike, 3 at the end. Palette `battleDawn`. Music: Marwa, drone only while choosing; on the reveal, percussion, strings and climax (conch once at the strike), then Yaman resolution for the laps.

**Beat 8: Mother of Skanda** — picture: the Devi's warm glow with its faint head-and-shoulders outline, seated on a lotus; the child-light on her lap facing outward; around her six small gold outline icons: the child, two lotuses, a raised open hand, the lion, the lotus seat, a throat-chakra circle. Each icon is a hotspot. For the Tamil step, a slim gold vel icon passes from her glow to the child-light. Camera pushIn, slow. Intensity 2. Palette `motherGold`. Music: indian, Bhupali, 60 bpm; drone, motif (the phrase whole), resolution bell on each hotspot. Ambience: none.

**Beat 9: The theme (Nurture)** — a faint image behind the words: a small diya cupped in two outline hands, the flame leaning outward through an open doorway of light. Music: Yaman, drone and soft motif.

**Beat 10: The argument at Grimmauld Place** — letterbox. Picture: a long dark kitchen table, candles in a row; sentence 1, chairs on both sides, no figures, just shadows; sentences 2–4, two candle flames on opposite sides flare and lean away from each other as the argument goes back and forth; sentence 5, a third candle steadies between them; sentence 6, a small candle at the far end is snuffed (Ginny sent to bed); sentence 8, the candles dim one by one. Camera slow pan along the table. Freeze after sentence 4. Intensity 4. Palette `grimmauldKitchen`. Music: hogwarts, low woodwind and soft strings, 64 bpm; celesta bell on sentence 5. Ambience: quiet fire crackle.

**Beat 11: Think it through** — backdrop: the table, candles low. Music: hogwarts drone; the activity ducks the score to the drone, with the timer tick.

**Beat 12: Narcissa's lie** — letterbox. Picture: night forest, bare trunks, a clearing in moonlight; sentence 0, a pale low shape on the ground suggested only by moonlight on leaves; sentence 3, a single lantern-amber point kneels close and a tiny pulse of light beats twice beneath it (the heartbeat); sentence 4, the lantern point leans closer and dims; sentence 5, it rises; sentence 6, far off between the trees, a castle window glows. Camera lowAngle, still. Slow motion on sentence 3 (2 s). Freeze after sentence 5. Intensity 6. Palette `forestNight`. Music: hogwarts, soft strings and harp, 56 bpm; harp pulse matching the heartbeat; no climax layer. Ambience: one soft gust in the trees. Parent bell at the start and before sentence 1.

**Beat 13: Think it through** — backdrop: the clearing, the castle window glowing. Music: hogwarts drone and harp.

**Beat 14: Side by side** — backdrop: faint night sky with the spark and six stars. Music: Yaman drone.

**Beat 15: Today** — backdrop: a lit house window at night. Music: Yaman, drone and soft motif.

**Beat 16: Reflection** — backdrop: the lotus lake before dawn, the Devi's glow faint. 16.2 brightens to dawn. Music: Yaman, resolution layer; one temple bell at the theme.

**The closing (about 13 seconds):** dawn over the lotus lake; the Devi's glow with the child-light on her lap, facing the horizon. The theme's word and idea fade in large and hold. The spark lifts from her lap and flies out of frame to the map, where diya 5 lights. Music: the Yaman phrase whole, one bell, a 5 s tanpura tail.

## New drawing pieces

- **Spark:** a small bright point with a faint trail, able to travel along a path and be "held" by other lights.
- **SmokeDemon:** a drifting dark smoke shape with ember points that can spread, mass and dissolve.
- **DivineLight colours:** add gold-white (Brahma), ember (Agni), pale violet (Indra).
- **River of light** with reeds (Ridge-like layer of reed stalks that part).
- **Star cluster:** six linked stars (Pleiades), reusable for other nights' skies.
- **Devi silhouette:** a faint fine-line head-and-shoulders outline inside a Glow (new rule, 18 October), reusable on later nights.
- **SymbolIcon additions:** child-light with six points, raised open hand (abhaya), lion, spear (vel), discus, thunderbolt, elephant, snake, throat-chakra circle, dove.
- **Hogwarts props:** long table with candle row; forest clearing with a lantern point and a distant lit window.
