I checked about 75 factual claims in the lesson, the read-aloud script and the sources table. They cover the Bhagavata Purana 4.2–4.7, Shiva Purana Parvati Khanda ch. 5, the Kena Upanishad, Navaratri practice, the history of the ban on sati, and the Harry Potter references (PoA ch. 2–3, PS ch. 5–7, CoS ch. 18, the DH epilogue, and the films). The traditional story is accurate, with good verse references. Every BP detail I checked against vedabase.io matches. The Harry Potter retellings have a few small errors of fact or attribution. Problems: **High 0, Medium 3, Low 6.**

## 3. Resolved *to check* items

- **Shiva Purana chapter for the rebirth to Mena (sources table: "medium (Shiva Purana chapter, from an index)").** Confirmed. In J. L. Shastri's translation, Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda **ch. 5 is "Menā obtains the boon"**. Himavat and Menā do penance together (vv. 4–5). Menā worships Śivā for 27 years (v. 7), asks for a hundred sons and for Śivā as her daughter (vv. 34–36), and the Goddess says "I shall be born as your daughter" (v. 39). Ch. 6 is Parvati's birth. You can raise the confidence to High. Evidence: https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226069.html and the chapter index https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226064.html
- **Beat 10 Film note ("Compare how the film handles the deal over the Hogsmeade form").** Unverified. I could not confirm from a reliable source how the 2004 film presents the form deal, so the existing caution in the doc should stay. The film does keep Vernon's "unemployed" line, Marge's insults, the inflation and the Knight Bus. That matches the Film note, but I confirmed it from memory and fan sources, not a primary source (medium confidence).
- **"Film wording may differ" (CoS 2002).** Confirmed. The film reverses the order of the line ("not our abilities... it is our choices"), and the idea is kept.

## 4. Theme (not to be changed)

No factual problems. The theme says "Devotees often link Shailaputri with the mountain's steadiness" and "named for a relationship" (daughter of the mountain). Both are consistent with her name and with common devotional readings.

## 5. Checked and correct

- *Nava* = nine, *ratri* = night. Sharada Navaratri is in Ashvin (Sept/Oct) and ends with Vijayadashami/Dussehra. (Britannica, "Navratri")
- The Navadurga names come from the Devi Kavacham ("prathamam Shailaputri..."), recited with the Devi Mahatmya. The Mahatmya does tell the Mahishasura episode, along with others. (wisdomlib Devi Kavacham / Devi Mahatmya)
- Regional customs (garba and dandiya, Durga Puja, Golu, Ramlila, Ghatasthapana with sprouting grain) are accurate. (Britannica)
- BP 4.2.6: all rose for Daksha except Brahma and Shiva. 4.2.16: he gave his daughter "on the request of Lord Brahma". 4.2.18: the curse denying Shiva a share. https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/4/2/
- BP 4.3.3–10: the sacrifice without Shiva, the demigods' wives passing in airplanes in finery, Sati longing for her sisters and relatives. 4.3.13–18: you may go uninvited unless the host is angry. 4.3.19: relatives' harsh words hurt more than enemies' arrows. 4.3.20: she will not be honoured. https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/4/3/
- BP 4.4.7–10: mother and sisters welcome her with tears, Daksha does not receive her, no oblations for Shiva, Sati stops the attendants. 4.4.11–15: Shiva has no enemies and is friend of all. 4.4.12: he finds no faults. 4.4.22: ashamed of the bodily relationship. **4.4.23: the "Dakshayani" name (citation correct).** 4.4.24: sits facing north. 4.4.26–27: yogic fire. 4.4.28–30: uproar, and Daksha blamed. https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/4/4/
- BP 4.5.1–3: Narada brings the news, Shiva dashes down a strand of hair, and Virabhadra arises (named from v. 17). https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/4/5/
- BP 4.7.3: goat's head. 4.7.11–15: Daksha weeps and asks forgiveness. **4.7.58–59: reborn to Mena in the Himalayas and accepts Shiva again (citation correct).** https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/4/7/
- Shiva carrying Sati's body and the Shakti Peethas are in later texts (Devi Bhagavata Purana 7.30, Kalika Purana). Versions differ: in the Kalika Purana the gods enter the corpse, rather than Vishnu cutting it. "Versions vary" covers this. Kamakhya (Assam) and Kalighat (Kolkata) are Shakti Peethas. (Britannica "Kamakhya"; Wikipedia "Shakti Peetha")
- *Shaila* = mountain/rock, *putri* = daughter. Parvati = "she of the mountain". Iconography: bull, trident in her right hand, lotus in her left, crescent moon. The Muladhara link is a yogic interpretation (correctly labelled).
- Kena Upanishad 3.12: Uma Haimavati appears to Indra. 4.1: she reveals that the victory was Brahman's. https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/kena-upanishad-shankara-bhashya
- Bengal Sati Regulation 1829 and the Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act 1987. Ram Mohan Roy campaigned against sati. (Britannica "suttee")
- PoA ch. 2 is "Aunt Marge's Big Mistake": the summer of his 13th birthday, Marge's week-long stay, the final dinner, "good-for-nothing", the car-crash insult, Marge inflating to the ceiling, Harry leaving with his trunk fearing expulsion, the form never signed. (Muggles' Guide PoA ch. 2)
- PoA ch. 3 "The Knight Bus": Fudge says Marge was deflated and her memory modified.
- PS ch. 7 "The Sorting Hat": the Hat sees courage, a good mind, talent and a thirst to prove himself, and says Slytherin would help him to greatness; Harry thinks "not Slytherin"; Gryffindor. Hagrid's "every wizard who went bad was in Slytherin" line is in PS ch. 5.
- CoS ch. 18 "Dobby's Reward": Parseltongue doubts, and "our choices... far more than our abilities".
- DH epilogue ("Nineteen Years Later"): Harry tells Albus Severus that the Hat takes your choice into account.
- Films: PS 2001, CoS 2002, PoA 2004. PoA opens with the Marge dinner and the Knight Bus.
- Snape, Regulus Black and Slughorn as Slytherins who complicate the house picture: correct.
