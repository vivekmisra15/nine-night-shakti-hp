# Fact-check: Night 2 (docs/nights/night-2.md)

## 1. Summary

I checked about 110 factual claims in the lesson, the read-aloud script and the sources table. The Shiva Purana claims were checked verse by verse against J. L. Shastri's translation on wisdomlib.org (Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda, chapters 11–28). The Kalidasa citations were checked against the GRETIL Sanskrit text of the *Kumarasambhava*. The Harry Potter claims were checked against the Wikibooks *Muggles' Guide* and the Harry Potter Wiki. The traditional story is very accurate: every cited chapter and verse range holds up, including ch. 22 vv. 18–27 (Uma), vv. 47–49 (Aparna), vv. 40–45 and 67–71, and ch. 28 vv. 36–50. The Harry Potter retellings contain no invented details. I found **9 problems: 1 High, 1 Medium and 7 Low.** The High one is that the text says Shiva "had no reply" to Parvati's argument, when in fact he answers her and then lets her serve him. The Medium one is a chapter citation that is out by one. The Low items are imprecise wording or film details I could not confirm. There is one small factual point in the theme, listed in section 4.

## 3. Resolved *to check* items

- **Taraka's boon, exact chapter** (sources table: "medium (exact chapter of the boon)"): the boon is in **Parvati Khanda ch. 15, vv. 38–41**. Taraka asks for two boons; the second is that he may die only when "a son born of Śiva becomes the commander-in-chief of an army and discharges weapons against me". Ch. 14 covers his birth and penance. You can raise the confidence to High and cite "ch. 15 (boon); ch. 17 (Kama sent)". https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226079.html
- **Prisoner of Azkaban film compresses the lessons** (Medium, "from transcript"): confirmed. The film has one lesson, in the Astronomy Tower set, and he succeeds on the second attempt. https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-Dementor_lessons
- **Order of the Phoenix film: memory seen through a spell backfiring, not the Pensieve** (Medium): confirmed (Shield Charm). "Two lessons" and "no Kreacher lie" are consistent with my recollection of the film but not confirmed in a source. https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_Severus_Snape%27s_private_lessons
- **Goblet of Fire film omits the practice** (Medium): consistent with the film as I recall it, where Harry summons the Firebolt in the arena only. I found no source that states it explicitly, so this stays **Unverified**.
- **Alternative ladder and activity marked "new, for review"**: these are questions, not factual claims, so there is nothing to check.

## 4. Theme (not to be changed)

- "Parvati first tried the quick way, with Kama's arrow and her own beauty": in the Shiva Purana, Kama's arrow was the gods' plan. Indra sent Kama at Brahma's bidding (ch. 13, v. 56; ch. 17). Parvati's own earlier way was daily service at her father's request (ch. 12–13), not the arrow. As an interpretation it can stand, and Beat 4 says it correctly ("the gods' plan used her beauty"). I raise it only so the owner knows; I suggest no change.
- "Her name means 'one who practises'": this is a fair short gloss of *brahmacharini*. Beat 1 labels the fuller meaning as interpretation. There is no problem.

- Canto 5: Aparna (leaf-only diet, then none), the matted-haired ascetic "like the first ashrama (brahmacharya) embodied" (5.30), and "from today… I am your slave, bought by your tapas" (5.86). Canto 3: Kama's burning.

Devotional:
- Devi Kavacham: "prathamaṃ śailaputrī ca dvitīyaṃ brahmacāriṇī", so Brahmacharini is the second Navadurga.
- Brahmacharini's iconography (white, barefoot, rosary and water pot, no mount) is standard devotional iconography. "Most Navadurga forms ride a lion, a bull or a tiger" is broadly correct (Kalaratri's donkey is the main exception).
