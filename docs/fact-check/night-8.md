## 1. Summary

I checked about 70 factual claims across the lesson, the read-aloud script and the sources table. They cover the Skanda Purana main telling verse by verse (Tagare translation on wisdomlib), the Kalika Purana, Shiva Purana, Matsya Purana and Devi Mahatmya variants, Mahagauri's iconography, regional practice, and the Harry Potter chapter titles, scenes and film notes. The traditional story holds up well. The Skanda Purana chapter matches the doc closely, including the verse ranges. All four *to check* items could be resolved: the Shiva Purana section is Vayaviya Samhita, part 1, chapters 24–25; the Matsya quarrel is ch. 156–157; and both film questions are settled. I found **no High**, **1 Medium** and **4 Low** problems. The Medium one is the read-aloud script's claim that the Kreacher chapters are "not in the films at all", which contradicts the lesson's own film note. The Low ones are small wording and citation points.

## 3. Resolved *to check* items

1. **Shiva Purana section (Beat 7; sources table).** Resolved. The section is Shiva Purana, Vayaviya Samhita part 1 (Purvabhaga), ch. 24, "Shiva's sports on the Mandara mountain", and ch. 25, "The goddess attains fair complexion". In ch. 24, Shiva calls her "Kali" in jest (vv. 31–32, 41) and she resolves on tapas to Brahma to become Gauri (v. 53). In ch. 25, Brahma asks for her help against Shumbha and Nishumbha (v. 36), her dark sheath becomes Kaushiki (v. 39), and she becomes white (v. 38). This matches Wikipedia's citation of Shastri, *Siva Purana* Part 4, pp. 1857–1865, which is the Vayaviya Samhita. https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226660.html ; https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226661.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahagauri
2. **Matsya Purana quarrel, "around ch. 156–157".** Resolved, with medium confidence. A published chapter list gives ch. 156 as the quarrel between Shiva and Parvati and ch. 157 as Parvati's curse on Viraka and her boon to Ekanamsha; the complexion episode runs through these chapters. I could not read the full text. Suggest "Matsya Purana ch. 155–158" with confidence Medium. https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/IDJ548/ (chapter listing, via search snippet)
3. **How much of Kreacher's tale the film includes.** Resolved. *Deathly Hallows Part 1* has a brief scene: Kreacher is summoned and questioned about Regulus and the locket, and Kreacher and Dobby bring in Mundungus. There is no cave flashback, no gift of the fake locket and no transformation. Beat 10's note is correct; the script line needs a fix (see table). https://www.mugglenet.com/?p=73011 ; https://www.honolulumagazine.com/?p=413759
4. **The Deluminator in the film (Beat 12 film note).** Resolved: it is in the film. Ron explains in the tent that on Christmas morning, while hiding in a pub from Snatchers, he heard Hermione's voice say his name from the Deluminator. He clicked it, and a small ball of light went into his chest and led him to them. The film's locket also uses both of the fears in the retelling: the mother who wanted a daughter, and the girl who prefers his friend. https://www.kinoafisha.info/en/movies/7667823/quotes/ (user-submitted quote page, consistent with my own memory of the film); https://www.hp-lexicon.org/thing/deluminator/
