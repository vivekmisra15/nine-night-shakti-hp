## Decisions

### Across the nights

1. **Choices on a short evening.** Nights 2, 3, 5, 6 and 8 each had to keep a choice's "What happens", because the story breaks without it (e.g. Night 5: Taraka is only defeated inside a choice). **Recommendation:** one rule for every night, Night 1 included: on a short evening each choice shows its set-up and what happened, without the question. Adds about a minute.
2. **Gentle alternatives and the screens after them.** Side by side and "What doesn't compare" (Nights 2, 3, 7) and Night 9's look-back name the intense scene, so they read wrongly if a family chose the gentle one. **Recommendation:** I write alternative versions of just those lines, in the docs first, marked new, for you to check at handover.
3. **Thin alternative retellings.** Night 2's Summoning Charm (about 80 words) and Night 7's Grim (about 70) were written only from the few details in the docs. **Recommendation:** I expand each to about 8 sentences from the books' chapters (*Goblet of Fire* ch. 20; *Prisoner of Azkaban* ch. 17–19), paraphrased and marked for checking.
4. **Night 9's look-back** recalls each night by its old question, and several don't match those nights' own question lines. **Recommendation:** recall each night by its theme word and one-line idea instead.
5. **Three new ragas.** Malkauns (deep night: Nights 2, 4, 7), Durga (the Devi taking form: Nights 3, 6) and Bhairav (dawn: Night 9, and optionally Night 8's close). **Recommendation:** add all three; each is well known and has a clear mood.
6. **The Devi's light-touch silhouette.** The briefs show a faint head-and-shoulders outline inside her light at key moments. **Recommendation:** add the same to Night 1's Shailaputri, for consistency.

### Themes (your wording; I haven't changed any of it)

7. **Night 8, Renewal (please look at this one).** The description follows the popular Ganga-washing telling, which the night itself labels "not found in a Purana". "The ringing overwhelms demons" needs a source (the doc cites only the gods' prayer, DM 4.24). **Recommendation:** "weapons and symbols of peace in her hands"; check the bell line's source.
10. **Night 4, Creation.** The theme is about why you make something, but the scenes (Hagrid's cake, the jumper) were chosen for warmth, so the connection lines no longer tie them to the theme. **Recommendation:** keep the theme, and let me reword the two connection lines around "a new world can begin with one warm act", marked new.

The description also cites the Devi Gita and the Devi Mahatmya's "cause of liberation", which aren't in the sources table. **Recommendation:** keep the theme, reword the connection lines to link power held lightly to wholeness (nothing left to grasp), and add the two sources once checked.
